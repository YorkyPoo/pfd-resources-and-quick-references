# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Scene Safety / PPE / Patient Contact:
• Utilize appropriate agencies and resources as needed (lift assistance, ALS, law enforcement).
• Bring all necessary equipment to the patient’s side.
2 EMR Initial Assessment:
• Perform Primary and Secondary Survey as indicated.
• Consider spinal motion restriction if indicated by MOI or history.
3 EMR Ensure Adequate Airway, Oxygenation, and Ventilation:
• Manage airway by the least invasive means necessary.
• Maintain home oxygen unless symptoms indicate need for increase.
• Administer up to 15 LPM oxygen through an appropriate delivery device.
4 EMR Vital Signs:
• Obtain BP, pulse rate, respirations, and GCS.
• Consider obtaining blood glucose level and temperature.
• Reassess Stable patients at least every 15 minutes.
• Reassess Unstable patients at least every 5 minutes.
5 EMT Assess SpO2:
• Consider assessing EtCO2 and/or SpCO as indicated.
6 EMT Consider 12-Lead ECG:
• Acquire within 5 minutes of patient contact.
• Transmit to receiving facility within 10 minutes of patient contact.
7 EMT Medication Administration:
• Confirm all "Rights" (Patient, Medication, Dose, Route, Time).
• Ensure medications are not expired.
• Assess for drug-drug interactions and contraindications.
8 A Establish IV / IO access:
• Establish access when indicated.
9 I Consider continuous cardiac monitoring.
10 EMT Transport:
• Base transport decisions on clinical condition and regional Field Triage policies.
• Deliver a pre-arrival report to the receiving facility as soon as practical.
• Consult with Medical Control regarding destination or clinical guidance.
11 EMR Refer to relevant protocols:
• If no protocol applies, or for clinical guidance at any time, contact Medical Control.
