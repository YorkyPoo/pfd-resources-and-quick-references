# Level Action / Intervention
1 PENDING Scene Safety / Initial Assessment:
• Follow the Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
• Perform general patient management.
2 PENDING Airway / Respiratory Management:
• Support life-threatening problems associated with airway, breathing, and circulation.
• If respiratory distress, refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support procedure and the Respiratory Distress protocol.
• Assess SpO2.
3 PENDING Oxygen Administration:
• Administer oxygen to maintain SpO2 94-99%.
4 PENDING Vital Signs / 12-Lead ECG:
• Obtain VS in both arms and assess distal pulses.
• Place patient on monitor and obtain (BLS) / interpret (ALS) 12-lead ECG.
• Refer to the Chest Pain / ACS protocol as needed.
• DO NOT administer Aspirin if acute MI is present in conjunction with suspected AAA or aortic dissection.
5 PENDING Glucose Assessment:
• Check blood glucose level.
• Refer to the Hyperglycemia / Hypoglycemia protocol if indicated.
6 PENDING IV/IO Access:
• Establish two IV of normal saline and titrate to a systolic B/P > 90 mmHg.
• Do not delay transport to establish second IV.
• Refer to Intraosseous Access procedure if IV access is difficult.
7 PENDING Pain Management:
• Administer Fentanyl 1 mcg/kg IN/IV/IM or Morphine Sulfate 2.5-5.0 mg IV/IM as needed, per the Pain Control protocol.
• Confirm all "Rights" prior to administration (Right patient, medication, dose, route, time).
8 PENDING Nausea Management:
• Administer Ondansetron (Zofran) 0.1 mg/kg slow IVP as needed per the Nausea and Vomiting protocol.
9 PENDING Hypotension Management - Vasopressors:
• Consider Norepinephrine (Levophed) Infusion 0.1-0.5 mcg/kg/minute for hypotension.
• Titrate to systolic B/P > 90 mmHg.
• Refer to Levophed Dosing Chart below.
• Administer vasopressors through the largest, most proximal IV site available (AC or higher preferable) or IO. Verify patency prior to initiating infusion and monitor closely for signs of extravasation.
10 PENDING Transport:
• Transport and perform ongoing assessment as indicated.
• Do not delay transport for any reason if possible; interventions should be done enroute to appropriate facility.
11 PENDING Medical Control Contact:
• Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed.
