# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Scene Safety / Initial Contact:
• Ensure scene safety and appropriate PPE.
• Determine DNR status (refer to Death and Criteria for Withholding Resuscitation protocol).
• Patients with valid durable DNR or injuries inconsistent with survival are excluded.
2 EMR Begin High-Quality CPR:
• Push hard and fast at rate 100 – 120 bpm.
• Allow complete chest recoil.
• Minimize interruptions in chest compressions.
• Change compressors every 2 minutes or sooner if fatigued.
3 EMT Airway Management:
• Apply BVM with high-flow O₂.
• Use quantitative EtCO₂ monitoring with BVM:
- If EtCO₂ < 10 mmHg, improve compressions
- If sudden increase above 30-40 mmHg, check for ROSC
• Give continuous compressions with advanced airway.
4 EMR AED / Rhythm Assessment:
• Apply the AED and repeat the AED rhythm analysis every 2 minutes; the AED announces whether a shock is advised.
4a I† • Interpret the rhythm: V-Fib/V-Tach (shockable) or Asystole/PEA (non-shockable).
5 I Defibrillation - V-Fib/V-Tach Only:
• ADULT: 200 joules Biphasic / 360 joules Monophasic, or manufacturer's recommendation
• PEDIATRIC: 2 joules/kg initial, 4 joules/kg subsequent
• Ensure optimal pad placement; consider adjustment if multiple shocks delivered without rhythm change.
5a EMR • After each defibrillation, perform 2 minutes of CPR, then reassess rhythm.
6 EMT Spinal Motion Restriction:
• Consider spinal motion restriction if indicated.
7 A IV/IO Access:
• Obtain IV/IO access (humeral IO preferred if IV unavailable).
• Administer fluids and medications.
8 A Epinephrine - All Rhythms:
• ADULT: 1 mg IV/IO [0.1 mg/mL concentration]
• PEDIATRIC: 0.01 mg/kg IV/IO [0.1 mg/mL concentration]
• Frequency: Repeat every 3–5 minutes during cardiac arrest
• Administer without interruption of CPR.
9 I Amiodarone - V-Fib/V-Tach Only (if persists after defibrillation and Epinephrine):
• ADULT: 300 mg IV/IO slow push
• PEDIATRIC: 5 mg/kg IV/IO slow push. Maximum dose 300 mg.
• Alternative if Amiodarone unavailable: Contact Medical Control for guidance
10 A Fluid Administration - Asystole/PEA Only:
• ADULT: 0.9% Sodium Chloride up to 1 L in 250 – 500 mL boluses
• PEDIATRIC: 0.9% Sodium Chloride 20 mL/kg
• Repeat as needed to maintain adequate perfusion, up to 60 mL/kg total.
11 I Calcium Chloride and Sodium Bicarbonate - Special Circumstances Only:
(For cardiac arrest in renal dialysis patients, crush injuries, suspected calcium channel blocker overdose, or suspected hyperkalemia)
• ADULT:
- Calcium Chloride 1 g IV/IO slow push, followed by 20 mL 0.9% sodium chloride flush
- Sodium Bicarbonate 50 mEq IV/IO slow push
• PEDIATRIC:
- Calcium Chloride 20 mg/kg (max dose 1g) IV/IO slow push, followed by 20 mL 0.9% sodium chloride flush
- Sodium Bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg (max dose 50 mEq) IV/IO slow push
• May repeat in 5 minutes if no change.
12 EMR Identify and Treat Reversible Causes:
• Continuously assess for H's and T's throughout resuscitation.
• Treat underlying cause if identified.
13 MC Termination of Resuscitation (TOR):
Contact Medical Control to request TOR if ALL criteria met:
• Arrest was not witnessed by EMS
• After 20 minutes of continuous CPR with no ROSC
• AND no shocks were delivered
14 EMR Post-Resuscitation:
• If ROSC achieved, refer to Post Cardiac Arrest Care (ROSC) protocol.
• If ROSC not achieved, refer to Death and Criteria for Withholding Resuscitation protocol.
