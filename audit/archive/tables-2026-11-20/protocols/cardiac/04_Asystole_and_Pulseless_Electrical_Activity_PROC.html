# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol. Determine DNR status. Refer to Death and Criteria for Withholding Resuscitation protocol. Consider spinal motion restriction.
2 EMR Begin chest compressions. Refer to Adult / Pediatric Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) procedure. Children/Infants ONLY: If unconscious and hypotensive, with HR less than 60, begin chest compressions. Alternate compressors every 2 minutes or sooner if fatigued. Consider mechanical CPR device, if available.
3 EMR Identify and treat potentially reversible causes. See Pearls below.
4 EMR Ensure adequate oxygenation. Refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support procedure.
5 EMT Obtain and transmit a 12-Lead ECG.
6 A Obtain IV/IO access.
7 EMT Monitor ETCO2. If ETCO2 < 10 or dropping, reassess and optimize compression rate, depth, and recoil.
8 EMR Apply the AED and repeat the AED rhythm analysis every 2 minutes; the AED announces whether a shock is advised. If the AED is advising to shock, defibrillate at maximum joules.
8a I† Interpret the rhythm: VF or VT (shockable), or asystole or PEA (non-shockable).
8b I Manual defibrillation: if the rhythm is VF or VT, defibrillate immediately — ADULT: 200 J Biphasic / 360 J Monophasic; PEDIATRIC: 2 J/kg initial, then 4 J/kg subsequent. Then refer to Cardiac Arrest: Ventricular Fibrillation / Pulseless Ventricular Tachycardia protocol.
9 A Children/Infants ONLY: If unconscious and hypotensive, with HR less than 60, administer:
PEDIATRIC: Epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg IV/IO [0.1 mg/mL] every 3 – 5 minutes, as needed.
10 I For cardiac arrest in renal dialysis patients, patients with crush injuries, suspected calcium channel blocker overdose, or suspected hyperkalemia only, also administer:
ADULT: Calcium Chloride 1 g IV/IO slow push followed by 20 mL 0.9% sodium chloride flush.
Sodium Bicarbonate 50 mEq IV/IO slow push.
Repeat in 5 minutes if no change.
11 I For cardiac arrest in renal dialysis patients, patients with crush injuries, suspected calcium channel blocker overdose, or suspected hyperkalemia only, also administer:
PEDIATRIC: Calcium Chloride 20 mg/kg (max dose 1 g) IV/IO slow push followed by 20 mL 0.9% sodium chloride flush.
Sodium Bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg (max dose 50 mEq) IV/IO slow push.
Repeat in 5 minutes if no change.
12 MC If ROSC is achieved, refer to Post Cardiac Arrest Care (ROSC) protocol.

If ROSC is not achieved, refer to Death and Criteria for Withholding Resuscitation protocol. Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed. Note: Once initiated, termination of resuscitative efforts is a Medical Control decision only.
