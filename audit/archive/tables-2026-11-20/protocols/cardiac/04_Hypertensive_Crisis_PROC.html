# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact:
• Follow the Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
• Ensure scene safety and utilize appropriate PPE.
2 EMR Primary Assessment:
• Perform Primary and Secondary Survey.
• Obtain complete set of vital signs including bilateral BP if possible.
• Assess for signs of end-organ damage (neurological, cardiac, pulmonary, renal).
3 EMR Stroke Assessment:
• Perform stroke screening (Cincinnati Prehospital Stroke Scale or equivalent).
• If stroke is suspected, refer to the Stroke / TIA protocol immediately.
• Note time of symptom onset if applicable.
4 EMR Cardiac Assessment:
• Assess for chest pain, pressure, or symptoms consistent with acute coronary syndrome.
• Obtain 12-lead ECG if available.
• If ACS is suspected, refer to the Chest Pain (Cardiac) protocol immediately.
5 EMR Respiratory Assessment:
• Assess for respiratory distress, pulmonary edema, or hypoxia.
• Apply pulse oximetry and administer oxygen as indicated.
• If respiratory distress is present, refer to the Respiratory Distress protocol and Airway Management and Ventilatory Support Procedure.
6 EMR Glucose Assessment:
• Check blood glucose level.
• If hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia is present or suspected, refer to the Hyperglycemia / Hypoglycemia protocol.
• Treat glucose abnormalities per appropriate protocol.
7 MC Medical Control Consultation:
• Contact Medical Control for guidance on blood pressure management as needed.
• Do NOT attempt to aggressively lower blood pressure in the field without specific orders.
• Report all associated symptoms, vital signs, and suspected target organ involvement.
