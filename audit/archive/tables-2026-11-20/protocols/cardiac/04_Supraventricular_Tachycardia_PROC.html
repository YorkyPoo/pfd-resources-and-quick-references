# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact
2 EMR Assess patient stability (chest pain, dyspnea, AMS, shock)
# Level Action / Intervention
4 EMT Obtain and transmit 12-Lead ECG
5 A Obtain IV access
6 A 0.9% Sodium chloride up to 1 L IV/IO (Adult)
250 – 500 mL boluses or 20 mL/kg IV/IO (Pediatric)
Repeat as needed, max 60 mL/kg total
7 I If rhythm regular, Adenosine (Adenocard) 6 mg IV/IO (Adult)
or 0.1 mg/kg IV/IO (Pediatric, max 6 mg)
Followed by 20 mL (Adult) or 10–20 mL (Ped) 0.9% Sodium chloride flush
8 I If no conversion after 2 minutes, repeat Adenosine at DOUBLE initial dose
9 I If rhythm irregular/atrial fibrillation, initiate transport
Monitor for signs of poor perfusion
10 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance
Ensure 12-Lead ECG transmitted
# Level Action / Intervention
12 I For light sedation, consider Midazolam (Versed)
2.5 mg IV/IO or 5 mg IN/IM (Adult)
0.05 mg/kg IV/IO or 0.1 mg/kg IM (Pediatric)
Max: 2.5 mg IV/IO or 5 mg IM
13 I Monitor SpO₂ and EtCO₂ during sedation
14 I Perform SYNCHRONIZED cardioversion
100 joules initial dose (Adult) or 1 joule/kg (Pediatric)
15 I If unsuccessful, check pad placement and repeat at 200 joules (Adult)
or 2 joules/kg (Pediatric)
16 I If unsuccessful after two attempts, contact Medical Control
