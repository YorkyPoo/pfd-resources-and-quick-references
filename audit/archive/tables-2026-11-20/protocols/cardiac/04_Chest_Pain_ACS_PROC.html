# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Scene Safety / Initial Assessment:
• Follow the Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
• If stroke suspected, refer to the Stroke / TIA protocol.
2 EMR Airway / Respiratory Management:
• If respiratory distress, refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support procedure and the Respiratory Distress protocol.
• Assess SpO2.
3 EMR Glucose Assessment:
• Check blood glucose level.
• Refer to the Hyperglycemia / Hypoglycemia protocol if indicated.
4 EMT 12-Lead ECG Acquisition:
• Consider assessing EtCO2 and/or SpCO as indicated.
• Acquire 12-Lead ECG within 5 minutes of patient contact.
• Transmit to receiving facility within 10 minutes of patient contact.
5 I 12-Lead ECG Interpretation - STEMI Criteria:
• Intermediate and Paramedic providers may interpret as STEMI if:
- Symptoms consistent with ACS/myocardial ischemia AND
- ≥ 1 mm ST segment elevation in two or more contiguous leads
6 EMT STEMI Alert / Notification:
• Contact receiving facility via COR and provide "Pre-Hospital STEMI Alert" with patient's name and date of birth to facilitate rapid registration.
• Do NOT provide patient identifiers over the HEAR.
• Consider destination per Administrative Policy - STEMI Field Triage if indicated.
7 MC Extended ECG Assessment:
• If 12-Lead ECG is indeterminate or high suspicion for cardiac ischemia, obtain serial ECGs at 10-minute intervals.
• Contact Medical Control for guidance.
8 A IV/IO Access:
• Establish IV / IO access when indicated (refer to Intraosseous Access procedure).
9 I Cardiac Monitoring:
• Consider continuous cardiac monitoring.
10 EMT Aspirin Administration (for ACS/STEMI):
• Aspirin (NSAID - inhibits platelet action preventing clot formation)
• Indication: Acute coronary syndromes (ACS)/STEMI
• Confirm all "Rights" (Right patient, medication, dose, route, time)
• Ensure medications not expired, assess for drug-drug interactions
• Contraindications: Allergy to Aspirin/NSAIDs
• Precautions: History of GI bleeding/peptic ulcers, chronic kidney disease, trauma
11 EMT Nitroglycerin Administration:
• Administer Nitroglycerin per the Pain Control protocol for chest pain.
• Confirm all "Rights" prior to administration.
12 I Analgesia:
• Consider analgesia (refer to the Pain Control protocol).
13 MC Medical Control Contact:
• Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed.
14 I+MC Right-Sided / 15-Lead ECG:
• Consider right-sided 12-Lead or 15-Lead ECG for additional assessment.
