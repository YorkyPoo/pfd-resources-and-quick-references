# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Control major active external bleeding / Begin CPR
2 EMT Open the airway / Ventilate
3 EMT Apply AED / Monitor (Assess rhythm before CPR if possible)
4 I Asystole on arrival: Consider withholding CPR
5 EMT PEA / VF / VT: Continue CPR
5a I Defibrillate if indicated
6 A Obtain IV/IO access (x2 preferred)
7 A If blood unavailable: 0.9% Sodium Chloride up to 1 L IV/IO (Adult) / 20 mL/kg IV/IO (Ped)
8 I Assess Shock Index > 1 / Resuscitate prior to intubation
9 I Perform bilateral chest decompression for tension pneumo
10 EMT Rapid transport to Level I/II Trauma Center (Goal < 10 mins)
11 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance / destination
