# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol
2 I Assess and continuously monitor cardiac rhythm
3 I Atropine sulfate
ADULT: 1 mg IV/IO
PEDIATRIC: 0.02 mg/kg IV/IO
Minimum dose: 0.1 mg, Maximum dose: 0.5 mg
May repeat once after 5 minutes, if needed
4 I If hypotensive or in shock, initiate transcutaneous pacing. Follow Transcutaneous Pacing procedure
5 I Maintain mean arterial pressure (MAP) above 65 mmHg. If pacing is ineffective, administer:
Epinephrine infusion 2 – 10 mcg/min IV/IO
(Add 1 mg of Epinephrine [1mg/mL] to 100 mL of 0.9% Sodium chloride = 10 mcg/mL)
6 I* Maintain age adjusted SBP. If pacing is ineffective, administer:
PEDIATRIC: Epinephrine 0.1 - 1 mcg/kg/min IV/IO infusion
Max dose: 10 mcg/min
Under 1 year old: Titrate to SBP > 70 mmHg
Over 1 year old: Titrate to SBP > 90 mmHg
7 P To maintain MAP > 65 until a vasopressor infusion is prepared, consider:
ADULT: Push dose Epinephrine 5-10 mcg IV/IO every 2-3 minutes
(Add 100 mcg Epinephrine (1mL of 0.1 mg/mL) to 9mL of 0.9% Normal Saline = 10 mcg/mL)
8 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed
Consider contacting Medical Control at a pediatric specialty center (VCU CHoR or CHKD) for children and infants with symptomatic bradycardia
