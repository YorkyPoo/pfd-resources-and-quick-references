# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Begin high-quality CPR / determine DNR status
2 EMT Consider spinal motion restriction
3 A Establish IV/IO (Humeral IO preferred)
4 A Fluid Resuscitation: 0.9% Sodium Chloride up to 1 L (Adult) / 20 mL/kg (Ped)
5 EMR Defibrillation — AED: apply the AED and defibrillate when the AED advises a shock (the device selects the energy).
5a I Defibrillation — Manual: 200 J Biphasic / 360 J Monophasic (Adult) / 2 J/kg initial, 4 J/kg subsequent (Ped)
6 A Epinephrine 1 mg (0.1mg/mL) IV/IO (Adult) / 0.01 mg/kg IV/IO (Ped) q3-5 mins
7 I First Antiarrhythmic: Amiodarone 300 mg IV/IO (Adult) / 5 mg/kg (Ped max 300mg) OR Lidocaine 1 mg/kg IV/IO (Adult max 100mg, Ped max 50mg)
8 I Second Antiarrhythmic: Amiodarone 150 mg IV/IO (Adult) / 5 mg/kg (Ped max 150mg, Medical Control authorisation required) OR Lidocaine 0.5 mg/kg IV/IO (Adult max 100mg) / 1 mg/kg (Ped max 50mg)
9 I Persistent VF/VT: Magnesium Sulfate 2 g IV/IO (Adult) / 40 mg/kg (Ped max 2g, Medical Control authorisation required)
10 I Dialysis/Crush/Hyperkalemia: Calcium Chloride 1 g IV/IO (Adult) / 20 mg/kg (Ped max 1g) THEN flush THEN Sodium Bicarbonate 50 mEq IV/IO (Adult) / 1 mEq/kg (Ped max 50 mEq)
11 EMR Proceed to Post ROSC Care if pulses return
12 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance / termination of resuscitation
