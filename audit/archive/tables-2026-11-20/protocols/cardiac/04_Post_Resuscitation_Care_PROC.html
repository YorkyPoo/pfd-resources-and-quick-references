# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Airway management / Ventilatory support
2 EMT Assess Blood Glucose (treat < 60 mg/dL per protocol)
3 EMT Obtain / transmit 12-lead ECG (STEMI Triage if indicated)
4 A Obtain IV/IO (Humeral IO preferred if IV unavailable)
5 A Fluid Bolus:
ADULT: 0.9% Sodium Chloride up to 1 L IV/IO in 250 - 500 mL boluses
PEDIATRIC: 0.9% Sodium Chloride 20 mL/kg IV/IO (up to 60 mL/kg total)
6 I Maintain MAP > 65 mmHg:
ADULT: Norepinephrine infusion 5 - 20 mcg/min IV/IO
OR
ADULT: Epinephrine infusion 2 - 10 mcg/min IV/IO
7 I* Pediatric SBP Support:
PEDIATRIC: Epinephrine infusion 0.1 - 1 mcg/kg/min IV/IO
(Titrate to > 70 mmHg if < 1 yr, > 90 mmHg if > 1 yr. Max dose: 10 mcg/min)
8 P Bridge Therapy:
ADULT: Push dose Epinephrine 5 - 10 mcg IV/IO every 2-3 minutes
