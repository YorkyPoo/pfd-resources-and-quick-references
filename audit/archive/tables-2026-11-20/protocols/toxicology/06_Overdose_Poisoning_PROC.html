# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care
2 EMR Remove from source / decontaminate patient
3 EMR Ensure adequate ventilation / administer supplemental oxygen
4 EMT Assess blood glucose / 12-lead ECG
5 EMT Opiate Overdose:
Administer Naloxone 1-2 mg IN
6 A Opiate Overdose (Adult):
Administer Naloxone 0.5-2 mg IV/IO/IM/IN

Opiate Overdose (Pediatric):
Administer Naloxone 0.1 mg/kg (max 2 mg) IV/IO/IM/IN
7 I Calcium Channel Blocker / Beta Blocker Overdose (Adult):
Administer Calcium Chloride 1g IV/IO AND Glucagon 1mg IV/IO

Calcium Channel Blocker / Beta Blocker Overdose (Pediatric):
Administer Calcium Chloride 20 mg/kg IV/IO AND Glucagon 0.5-1 mg IV/IO
8 I Organophosphate / Cholinergic Exposure (Adult & Pediatric >40kg):
Administer Atropine 2 mg IV/IO/IM

Organophosphate / Cholinergic Exposure (Pediatric <40kg):
Administer Atropine 1 mg IV/IO/IM
9 EMR Anti-Cholinergic / TCA Overdose:
Initiate active cooling measures
10 I Anti-Cholinergic / TCA Overdose (Adult):
Administer Sodium Bicarbonate 50 mEq IV/IO

Anti-Cholinergic / TCA Overdose (Pediatric):
Administer Sodium Bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg (max 50 mEq) IV/IO
