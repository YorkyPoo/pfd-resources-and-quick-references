# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care
2 EMR Don appropriate PPE; decontaminate patient prior to transport
3 EMR Follow Airway Management (Consider early intubation due to copious secretions)
4 EMR Refer to Nausea/Vomiting, Seizure, and Hypotension protocols as needed
5 EMT DuoDote Autoinjector IM (Adult and Pediatric ≥ 40kg). Repeat in 5 mins up to 3 doses based on symptom severity.
6 I Atropine sulfate 2 mg IV/IO/IM (Adult and Ped ≥ 40kg); 1 mg IV/IO/IM (Ped < 40kg). Repeat every 5 mins up to 3 doses. (Use if DuoDote unavailable)
7 MC Contact Medical Control for additional guidance and dosing instructions
