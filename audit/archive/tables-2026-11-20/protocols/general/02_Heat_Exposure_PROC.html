# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Place patient in cool environment
2 EMT Remove excess clothing
3 EMT Normal skin temp: Apply cool compresses
4 EMT Hot skin temp (Heat Stroke): Treat as life-threatening/start rapid cooling (cool mist, ice packs to armpits/groin/neck, wet sheet)
5 EMT Shivering: Slow the cooling process
6 A Suspected heat stroke/hypovolemia: 0.9% Normal Saline 20 mL/kg up to 1000 mL bolus IV/IO
7 EMT Continuously reassess patient
