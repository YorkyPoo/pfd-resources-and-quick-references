# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
2 EMR Assess baseline pain scale. Consider non-pharmacologic options (reassurance, distraction, positioning, splinting, cold therapy) first or as an adjunct.
3 EMT Assess and monitor blood pressure to ensure patient is not hypotensive (see criteria).
4 A Nausea:
• Adult: Ondansetron 4 mg IV/IM/PO
• Pediatric [> 1 year]: Ondansetron 0.15 mg/kg IV/IM/PO (Max dose: 4 mg)
5 A Mild to Moderate Pain (2 - 5):
• Adult: Ketorolac 15 mg IV / 30 mg IM
6 A* Moderate to Severe Pain (6 - 10):
• Adult: Fentanyl 25-50 mcg IV/IN/IM (Max total dose: 100 mcg)
• Pediatric: Fentanyl 0.5-1 mcg/kg IV/IN/IM (Max dose: 25 mcg, Max total dose: 100 mcg)
7 A* OR Moderate to Severe Pain (6 - 10):
• Adult: Morphine Sulfate 2.5-5 mg IV/IM (Max total dose: 10 mg)
• Pediatric: Morphine Sulfate 0.1 mg/kg IV/IM (Max dose: 2.5 mg, Max total dose: 10 mg)
8 MC If unable to adequately control pain or safely and humanely extricate/transport a stable patient, contact Medical Control for guidance.
