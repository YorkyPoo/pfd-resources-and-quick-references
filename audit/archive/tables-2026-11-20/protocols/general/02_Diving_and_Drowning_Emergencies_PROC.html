# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol. Remove the patient from the water.
2 EMR Consider spinal motion restriction. Refer to Spinal Motion Restriction procedure.
3 EMR Assess airway and ventilation. Refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support protocol.
4 EMR Remove wet clothing and dry the skin. Keep the patient warm.
5 EMR If hypothermia and hemodynamically unstable, focus effort on rewarming as well as resuscitation simultaneously.
6 EMR Diving emergencies: Assess for pulmonary barotrauma, decompression sickness, and/or arterial gas embolism. Refer to the appropriate protocol(s) as needed.
7 MC Contact Medical Control for hyperbaric transport destination guidance if decompression injury is suspected.
