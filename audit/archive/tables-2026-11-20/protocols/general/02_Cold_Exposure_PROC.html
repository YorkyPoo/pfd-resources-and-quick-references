# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow General – Universal Patient Care/Initial Patient Contact Protocol.
2 EMR Remove all wet clothing, place in a warm environment, prevent further heat loss, and replace with warm blankets.
3 EMR Handle the patient gently and avoid excessive movement (risk of cardiac arrest/VF).
4 EMR Apply warmed and humidified O2 if available.
5 EMR Localized cold injury: Apply loose, sterile dressings to the affected part.
7 EMR Cardiac Arrest: Make persistent attempts at resuscitation for victims of prolonged cold exposure. Begin CPR and continuous warming procedures.
8 EMT Rapid transport immediately with resuscitation efforts continued en route. Do not delay transport waiting for ALS.
