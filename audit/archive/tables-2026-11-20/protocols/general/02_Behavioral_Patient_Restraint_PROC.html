# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Assess capacity and treat potential underlying causes of agitation / altered mental status. Refer to administrative policy Patient Refusals.
2 EMR Apply de-escalation techniques. Remain calm, maintain soothing tone, allow space, do not reinforce delusions, set boundaries.
3 EMR Apply least restrictive physical restraints necessary. Refer to administrative policy Patient Restraint.
4 I As soon as feasible, assess and initiate continuous monitoring of cardiac rhythm.
5 I MODERATE AGITATION
Administer Haloperidol (Haldol) 5 mg IV/IO slow push or IM
(Pediatric: 0.02 mg/kg IV/IO slow push or 0.05 mg/kg IM. Max dose: 1 mg IV/IO, 2.5 mg IM)
(Use half dose 2.5 mg IV/IO or IM for frail, hypotensive, underweight <50kg, and elderly >65yrs)

AND

Administer Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 50 mg IV/IO slow push or IM
(Pediatric: 1 mg/kg IV/IO slow push or IM. Max dose 50 mg)
6 I SEVERE AGITATION (not responding to above)
Administer Midazolam (Versed) 5 mg IV/IO slow push or 10 mg IM
(Pediatric: 0.05 mg/kg IV/IO slow push or 0.1 mg/kg IN/IM. Max dose: 2.5 mg IV/IO, 5 mg IN/IM)
(Use half dose 2.5 mg IV/IO or 5 mg IM for frail, hypotensive, underweight <50kg, and elderly >65yrs)
⚠ Do not mix Haloperidol and Midazolam in the same syringe.
7 P IMMINENT HARM (Severely agitated with imminent risk of harm to self or others)
Administer Ketamine (Ketalar) 4 mg/kg IM
Be prepared to manage airway and support ventilation as needed.
8 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance if unable to safely restrain or transport the patient.
