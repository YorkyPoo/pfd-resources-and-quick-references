# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
2 EMR Ensure scene safety and that the patient is not energized (avoid second victim). Fully expose the injured area. Remove constricting items including clothing.
3 EMR If multiple patients are present: Treat cardiac arrest FIRST (reverse triage).
4 EMR Assess airway and respiratory effort. Ventilate using least invasive means necessary.
5 EMT Estimate TBSA and burn depth (only count 2nd and 3rd degree burns). 1% BSA = area of patient's palm.
6 EMT Apply clean, dry dressings to burns. Prevent loss of body heat. Keep the patient warm.
7 EMT Obtain and transmit a 12-Lead ECG
7a I Monitor rhythm.
8 A Establish IV/IO access on a non-burned extremity if possible (preferably x2 sites on non-burned skin). Only if necessary, obtain IV/IO access through a burned area.
9 A For an adult patient with SBP less than 90 and TBSA greater than 20%, administer fluid resuscitation:
Adult: 0.9% Sodium Chloride at 500 mL/hr IV/IO
Pediatric (6-12 years): 0.9% Sodium Chloride at 250 mL/hr IV/IO
Pediatric (≤5 years): 0.9% Sodium Chloride at 125 mL/hr IV/IO
