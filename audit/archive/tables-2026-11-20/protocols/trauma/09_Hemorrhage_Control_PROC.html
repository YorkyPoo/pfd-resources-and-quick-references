# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Care / Reassess bleeding after treatments performed.
2 EMR Head: Apply direct pressure / Hemostatic dressing (avoid eyes/mouth).
3 EMR Neck: Apply occlusive dressing / direct pressure (DO NOT pack).
4 EMR Extremity: Apply direct pressure/packing ➔ Tourniquet 2-3" above wound ➔ 2nd Tourniquet if needed.
5 EMR Junctional (Axilla or femoral): Pack tightly with hemostatic gauze / hold pressure x3 mins.
6 EMR Thoracoabdominal: Apply direct pressure / chest seal (DO NOT pack).
7 EMR Apply pelvic binder if unstable pelvis suspected.
8 EMR Consider pain control per the Pain Control protocol.
9 MC Contact Medical Control if hemorrhage cannot be controlled and transport immediately.
