# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
2 EMR Ensure scene safety.
2a EMT Extricate/move patient to safety if necessary.
3 EMR Maintain cervical spine stabilization/immobilization per Spinal Motion Restriction protocol.
4 EMT Assess airway and ventilatory support. Refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support procedure. Manage airway by the least invasive means necessary.
5 EMR Control hemorrhage. Refer to Hemorrhage Control protocol.
7 A Obtain IV/IO access.
8 A Fluid Resuscitation:
ADULT: 0.9% Sodium chloride IV/IO wide open. (Maximum 2L)
PEDIATRIC: 0.9% Sodium chloride 20mL/kg IV/IO. May repeat once if hypotensive.
9 I If considering intubation, assess Shock Index (Heart Rate / Systolic Blood Pressure). If Shock Index is greater than 1, consider resuscitation prior to intubation. Refer to Endotracheal Intubation procedure.
10 I Tension pneumothorax: Decompress the affected side(s). Refer to Chest Decompression procedure.
11 I Traumatic Cardiac Arrest: Refer to Cardiac Arrest: Traumatic protocol.
12 EMT If the patient meets the trauma triage criteria according to Administrative Policy – Trauma Field Triage, transport immediately to a Level 1 or Level II Trauma Center. Goal scene time: Less than 10 minutes.
13 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance and/or optimal destination determination as needed.
