# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol
2 EMR Ensure scene safety / patient not energized. Expose area and remove constricting items.
3 EMR Multiple patients: Treat cardiac arrest FIRST (reverse triage)
4 EMR Assess airway (manage by least invasive means necessary)
5 EMT Estimate TBSA/burn depth (2nd/3rd degree only)
6 EMT Apply clean dry dressings. Prevent loss of body heat and keep patient warm.
7 EMT Obtain 12-Lead ECG
7a I Monitor rhythm continuously
8 A Establish IV/IO (x2 preferred on non-burned skin)
9 A SBP < 90 & TBSA > 20%:
• Adult: 0.9% Sodium Chloride 500 mL/hr IV/IO
• Ped (6-12y): 0.9% Sodium Chloride 250 mL/hr IV/IO
• Ped (≤5y): 0.9% Sodium Chloride 125 mL/hr IV/IO
