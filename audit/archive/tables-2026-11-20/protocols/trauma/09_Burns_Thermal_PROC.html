# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Stop the burning process safely and remove the patient from the source. Fully expose the injured area and remove constricting items including clothing.
2 EMR Assess airway and respiratory effort. Refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support protocol.
3 EMT Estimate depth of burns and Total Body Surface Area (TBSA) involved using Rule of 9s or patient's palm (1%). Only count partial (2nd degree) and full thickness (3rd degree) burns.
4 EMT Apply clean, dry dressings to burns. Prevent loss of body heat and keep the patient warm.
5 A Establish IV/IO access on a non-burned extremity, if possible (preferably obtain two sites of access).
6 PENDING Hydrofluoric Acid (HF) Burns with Hypotension: Administer Calcium Chloride 1 g IV/IO over 3 mins (Repeat in 5 mins PRN).
7 MC Contact Medical Control if the most appropriate transport destination is unclear based on criteria.
