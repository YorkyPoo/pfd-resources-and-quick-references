# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol. Refer to Trauma, Hemorrhage Control, and Pain Control protocols as needed.
2 EMT If possible, obtain an ECG prior to and after release of the crushed part.
3 A Administer (when able, initiate prior to release):
- ADULT: 0.9% Sodium Chloride 1 L IV/IO in 250 - 500 mL boluses.
- PEDIATRIC: 0.9% Sodium Chloride 20 mL/kg IV/IO.
4 A+MC* Administer:
- ADULT / PEDIATRIC: Continuous Albuterol Sulfate (Proventil) 0.083% solution via nebulizer or BVM.
5 I+MC* Administer, preferably via separate lines (when able, initiate prior to release):
- ADULT / PEDIATRIC: Sodium Bicarbonate 1 mEq/kg IV/IO slow push. Maximum dose 50 mEq. Followed by 20 mL 0.9% Sodium Chloride flush.
For persistent ECG changes suggestive of crush syndrome or cardiac arrest, repeat once.
6 I* Administer, preferably via separate lines (when able, initiate prior to release):
- ADULT: Calcium Chloride 1 g IV/IO over 3 minutes.
- PEDIATRIC: Calcium Chloride 20 mg/kg IV/IO over 3 minutes. Maximum dose 1,000 mg (1 g).
For persistent ECG changes suggestive of crush syndrome or cardiac arrest, repeat once.
7 I Assess and continuously monitor cardiac rhythm.
8 EMT Extricate patient from entrapment and transport per Administrative Policy – Trauma Field Triage.
9 MC If it is anticipated the patient will not be able to be promptly and safely extricated, contact Medical Control at a regional trauma center for additional resources.
