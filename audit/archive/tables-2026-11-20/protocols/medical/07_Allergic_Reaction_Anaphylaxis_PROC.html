# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care:
• Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
2 EMT Stinger Removal:
• Remove stinger if present. Do not squeeze stinger. Scrape it away with a flat surface.
# Level Action / Intervention
3 A Antihistamine Administration:
• ADULT: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 50 mg IV/IO slow push or IM.
• PEDIATRIC: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 1 mg/kg IV/IO slow push or IM (Maximum dose: 50 mg).
4 A Bronchodilator (Wheezing):
• If wheezing is present, administer:
• ADULT & PEDIATRIC: Albuterol sulfate (Proventil) 3 mL of 0.083% solution, nebulized. Repeat as needed for wheezing.
5 A Anticholinergic (Wheezing):
• If wheezing is present, administer:
• ADULT & PEDIATRIC: Ipratropium bromide (Atrovent) 3 mL of 0.02% solution, nebulized, once.
6 A Corticosteroid (Consideration):
• Consider administering:
• ADULT: Methylprednisolone Succinate (Solu-Medrol) 125 mg slow IV push.
• PEDIATRIC: Methylprednisolone Succinate (Solu-Medrol) 2 mg/kg slow IV push (Maximum dose: 125 mg).
# Level Action / Intervention
7 EMT Epinephrine Administration:
• Administer IM using either dose-specific clearly marked or color-coded syringe.
• ADULT: Epinephrine autoinjector or 0.3 mg [1 mg/mL] IM.
• PEDIATRIC (< 30 kg): Pediatric Epinephrine autoinjector or 0.15 mg [1 mg/mL] IM.
• PEDIATRIC (> 30 kg): Epinephrine autoinjector or 0.3 mg [1 mg/mL] IM.
• Repeat every 5-15 minutes for up to 3 injections as needed.
8 A Vascular Access:
• Obtain IV/IO access.
9 A Fluid Resuscitation (Hypotension):
• If patient is hypotensive, administer:
• ADULT: 0.9% Sodium chloride up to 1 L IV/IO in 250 – 500 mL boluses.
• PEDIATRIC: 0.9% Sodium chloride 20 mL/kg IV/IO.
• Repeat as needed to maintain adequate perfusion, up to 60 mL/kg total.
10 A Antihistamine Administration:
• ADULT: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 50 mg IV/IO slow push or IM.
• PEDIATRIC: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 1 mg/kg IV/IO slow push or IM (Maximum dose: 50 mg).
11 A Bronchodilator (Wheezing):
• If wheezing is present, administer:
• ADULT & PEDIATRIC: Albuterol sulfate (Proventil) 3 mL of 0.083% solution, nebulized. May repeat as needed.
12 A Anticholinergic (Wheezing):
• If wheezing is present, administer:
• ADULT & PEDIATRIC: Ipratropium bromide (Atrovent) 3 mL of 0.02% solution, nebulized, once.
13 A Corticosteroid Administration:
• ADULT: Methylprednisolone succinate (Solu-Medrol) 125 mg IV/IO slow push.
• PEDIATRIC: Methylprednisolone succinate (Solu-Medrol) 2 mg/kg IV/IO slow push (Maximum dose: 125 mg).
