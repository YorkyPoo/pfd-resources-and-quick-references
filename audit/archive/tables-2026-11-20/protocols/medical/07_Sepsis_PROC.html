# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care
2 A Obtain IV/IO access and administer 0.9% Normal Saline up to 1 L in 250-500 mL boluses (Adult) or 20 mL/kg (Pediatric) max 60 mL/kg
3 I Maintain MAP > 65 mmHg. Norepinephrine infusion 5-20 mcg/min OR Epinephrine infusion 2-10 mcg/min (Adult)
4 I* Maintain age adjusted SBP. Epinephrine 0.1 - 1 mcg/kg/min infusion max 10 mcg/min (Pediatric)
5 P Push dose Epinephrine 5-10 mcg IV/IO every 2-3 mins to maintain MAP until infusion is ready (Adult)
6 MC Contact Medical Control / Prehospital Sepsis Alert
