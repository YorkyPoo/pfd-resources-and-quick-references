# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care, don appropriate PPE, and visualize perineum for crowning or presenting part.
2 EMR Normal Delivery: Apply gentle pressure on infant's skull to prevent explosive delivery. Check for nuchal cord. Dry, warm, and stimulate the neonate. DO NOT perform routine airway suctioning.
3 EMR Shoulder Dystocia: Place mother in McRobert's position (knees to chest) and apply suprapubic pressure.
4 EMR Umbilical Cord Prolapse: Wrap exposed cord in saline-moistened gauze. Elevate mother's hips or place in Trendelenburg position. Insert gloved hand to relieve pressure on the cord. DO NOT attempt vaginal delivery.
5 EMR Breech Delivery: Allow controlled, spontaneous delivery to the umbilicus. Deliver the legs and support the body.
6 I Eclampsia (Seizures): Administer Magnesium Sulfate 4 g IV/IO infusion over 10 minutes.
7 I If seizures persist > 5 minutes, administer Midazolam 5 mg IV/IO or 10 mg IM.
8 EMR Bleeding / Rupture / Abruptio: Place patient in left lateral recumbent position.
9 A Establish IV access and administer 0.9% Normal Saline 20 mL/kg up to 1,000 mL.
10 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance, especially for complications, eclampsia seizures, or difficult deliveries.
