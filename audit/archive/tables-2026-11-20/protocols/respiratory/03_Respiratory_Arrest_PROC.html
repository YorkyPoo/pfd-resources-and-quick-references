# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact:
• Follow the Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
• Assess for life threats and mechanism of respiratory failure.
2 EMT Position Patient and Open Airway:
• Position the patient appropriately (supine for intubation, recovery position if vomiting).
• Open the airway using head-tilt chin-lift or jaw-thrust maneuver.
3 EMT Continuous Monitoring:
• Apply continuous pulse oximetry (SpO2).
• Apply continuous box-waveform capnography (EtCO2) once airway placed.
4 EMT BLS Airway Adjuncts:
• Insert oropharyngeal airway (OPA) as indicated.
• Insert nasopharyngeal airway (NPA) if gag reflex present.
• Use supraglottic airway (SGA) as necessary.
5 EMR Suctioning:
• Provide suctioning of oropharynx as needed to clear secretions, blood, or vomitus.
• Pre-oxygenate before any airway manipulation.
6 EMT BVM Ventilation (Two-Person Technique):
• Support respirations with bag-valve-mask using two-person technique preferred.
• Deliver appropriate tidal volume (6-7 mL/kg) at rate of 10-12 breaths/min (adult).
• Avoid over-ventilation.
7 EMT EtCO2-Guided Ventilation:
• Use in-line EtCO2 monitoring during BVM ventilation.
• Adjust tidal volume, respiratory rate, and/or PEEP to maintain EtCO2 35–45 mmHg.
• Target 35-45 mmHg once advanced airway placed.
8 I Endotracheal Intubation – Adult (≥12 years):
• If apneic and unable to ventilate adequately by less invasive means, consider endotracheal intubation.
• Verify placement with continuous box-waveform capnography and auscultation.
9 P Endotracheal Intubation – Pediatric (<12 years):
• ⚠ RED DOT SKILL: Intubation of children <12 years is a Paramedic "red dot" skill only.
• If apneic and <12 years old, consider intubation if unable to ventilate adequately by less invasive means.
10 I Foreign Body Airway Obstruction:
• If foreign body airway obstruction suspected and unable to ventilate, perform laryngoscopy.
• Remove foreign body with Magill forceps under direct visualization.
11 P Surgical Airway (Cricothyrotomy):
• If unable to intubate and unable to oxygenate, proceed immediately to Surgical Airway.
• Use 14G, 3.25" catheter or surgical cricothyrotomy kit per protocol.
12 I Post-Intubation Airway Management:
• Verify tube placement with continuous box-waveform capnography AND auscultation (bilateral lung sounds, absence of gastric sounds).
• Secure tube with tape or commercial tube holder.
• Document depth of placement at teeth/gums.
13 EMT Ongoing Ventilation Monitoring:
• Monitor ET tube placement and ventilation continuously with SpO2 and EtCO2.
• Reassess lung sounds and gastric sounds after any patient movement.
14 I Post-Advanced Airway Analgesia – Fentanyl (Sublimaze):
• ADULT: 25–50 mcg IV/IO over 2 minutes or IN/IM.
• PEDIATRIC: 0.5–1 mcg/kg IV/IO over 2 minutes or IN/IM (Maximum dose 25 mcg).
• Repeat every 10–15 minutes as needed.
15 I Post-Advanced Airway Sedation – Midazolam (Versed):
• ADULT: 2.5 mg IV/IO over 2 minutes or 5 mg IN/IM.
• PEDIATRIC: 0.1 mg/kg IV/IO over 2 minutes or 0.2 mg/kg IN/IM (Maximum dose 2.5 mg IV/IO or 5 mg IN/IM).
• Repeat every 10–15 minutes as needed.
