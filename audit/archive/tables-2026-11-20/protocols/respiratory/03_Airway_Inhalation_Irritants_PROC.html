# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Scene Safety / PPE / Initial Assessment:
• Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
• Decision to enter contaminated area rests with incident commander and organizational policy.
2 EMT Decontamination (Gross):
• Victims must be decontaminated and/or confirmed "clean" prior to treatment and transport.
• Remove contaminated clothing.
• Brush off dry chemicals before irrigation.
3 EMT Airway Assessment:
• Assess for signs of airway injury (singed hairs, facial burns, hoarseness, stridor, soot).
• Monitor for progressive respiratory distress.
4 EMT Oxygen Administration:
• Apply high-flow oxygen via NRB or BVM as indicated.
• Target SpO₂ > 94%.
5 EMT Airway Management - Basic:
• Follow Airway Management and Ventilatory Support procedure.
• Position patient upright if tolerated.
6 A Albuterol Sulfate (Bronchodilator):
• Adult: 3 mL of 0.083% solution, nebulized; repeat as necessary
• Pediatric: 3 mL of 0.083% solution, nebulized; repeat as necessary
7 A Methylprednisolone (Solu-Medrol):
• Adult: 125 mg IV/IO slow push
• Pediatric: 2 mg/kg IV/IO slow push (Maximum dose: 125 mg)
8 A Sodium Bicarbonate - Chlorine Gas Exposure Only:
• Adult: 2 mL diluted with 2 mL 0.9% Sodium chloride, nebulized (ONCE only)
• Pediatric: 2 mL diluted with 2 mL 0.9% Sodium chloride, nebulized (ONCE only)
9 I Advanced Airway Assessment:
• Consider endotracheal intubation if signs of airway injury develop.
• Assess for impending airway compromise (worsening stridor, respiratory failure).
10 I Endotracheal Intubation:
• Follow Endotracheal Intubation procedure. Patients 12 years and older.
• ⚠ Intubation of spontaneously breathing patients without rapid sequence induction (RSI) is generally not recommended.
10a P • Orally intubate. Patients under 12 years old.
11 EMR Poison Control Consultation:
• Contact Poison Control Center at 1-800-222-1222 with any questions.
(Note: Poison Control is not online medical control.)
12 MC Medical Control Contact:
• Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed.
• Report agent exposure, interventions performed, and patient status.
