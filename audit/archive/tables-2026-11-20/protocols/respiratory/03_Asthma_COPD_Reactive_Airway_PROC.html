# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care / Assessment:
• Assess airway, breathing, circulation, and mental status.
• Apply pulse oximetry and cardiac monitoring.
• Assess severity: Moderate, Moderate to Severe, or Severe.
2 EMR Oxygen Administration:
• Administer oxygen to maintain SpO₂ ≥ 94%.
• Use nasal cannula, simple mask, or non-rebreather as appropriate.
3 EMT Albuterol Sulfate (Beta-Agonist):
• Adult & Pediatric: 3 mL of 0.083% solution via nebulizer.
• Repeat as necessary based on clinical response.
• May be administered continuously in severe cases per Medical Control direction.
4 EMT Ipratropium Bromide (Anticholinergic):
• Adult & Pediatric: 3 mL of 0.02% solution via nebulizer.
• ⚠ ONCE ONLY – Do NOT repeat without Medical Control consultation.
5 A Methylprednisolone (Solu-Medrol) for Moderate to Severe Symptoms:
• Adult: 125 mg IV/IO slow push.
• Pediatric: 2 mg/kg IV/IO slow push (maximum dose 125 mg).
• Administer if wheezing with moderate to severe respiratory distress.
6 I Magnesium Sulfate for Severe Symptoms / Impending Respiratory Failure:
• Adult: 2 g IV/IO infusion over 15 minutes.
• Pediatric: 40 mg/kg IV/IO over 15 minutes (maximum dose 2,000 mg or 2 g).
• Administer if severe bronchospasm unresponsive to initial therapy.
7 EMT Assist Ventilations / Airway Management:
• Prepare for assisted ventilation if respiratory failure develops.
• Bag-valve-mask with PEEP valve as indicated.
• Consider supraglottic airway or endotracheal intubation if unable to maintain adequate ventilation.
8 EMT Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP):
• Consider for COPD exacerbation or severe bronchospasm with adequate mentation.
• Initiate 5 cm H₂O.
• Contraindicated if altered mental status, vomiting, or respiratory arrest imminent.
8a I • Titrate PEEP up to 10 cm H₂O to support ventilation and work of breathing.
9 A Related Protocol – Stridor (Upper Airway Obstruction):
• Epinephrine: 3 mg [1 mg/mL solution] nebulized.
• May be repeated once if needed.
• Adult AND Pediatric same dosing.
• For anaphylaxis with stridor, follow Allergic Reaction Protocol.
10 A Methylprednisolone for Moderate to Severe Stridor:
• Same dosing as the methylprednisolone dose above.
• Administer after Epinephrine nebulization for persistent symptoms.
11 EMR Reassessment and Transport:
• Continuously monitor respiratory status, auscultation, and SpO₂.
• Reassess after each bronchodilator treatment.
11a EMT • Expedite transport for severe or deteriorating patients.
