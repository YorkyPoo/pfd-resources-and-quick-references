# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact
• Follow the Universal Patient Care protocol for all patients.
3 EMR Position of Comfort
• Allow child to assume position of choice (usually upright or with parent)
• Do not force supine positioning
• Head-down positioning for infants <1 year if foreign body suspected
4 EMR High-Flow Oxygen
• Apply high-flow oxygen via non-rebreather mask or blow-by
• Monitor SpO2 continuously
• Titrate to maintain SpO2 >94%
5 EMT Continuous Monitoring
• Pulse oximetry
• EtCO2 when available
5a I • Cardiac monitor
6 EMT CPAP for Upper Airway Support
• Consider CPAP for upper airway support if indicated
• Initial PEEP 5 cmH2O (titratable by I/P providers)
7 EMT Albuterol Nebulizer
• Albuterol 3 mL 0.083% solution nebulized
• May help with lower airway component
• Reassess after treatment
8 A Systemic Corticosteroids
• Methylprednisolone (Solu-Medrol) per dosing table below:
• Adult: 125 mg IV/IO slow push
• Pediatric: 2 mg/kg IV/IO slow push (max 125 mg)
• Steroids take 30-60 minutes to take effect
9 A IV/IO Access
• Obtain IV or IO access for medication administration
• Use largest appropriate catheter
• Do NOT delay Epinephrine for IV/IO placement
10 A Nebulized Epinephrine for Moderate to Severe Stridor
• Epinephrine 3 mg [1 mg/mL] nebulized
• May be repeated once if needed
• Same concentration as adults and pediatrics
11 P+MC Airway Protection - Advanced Airway
• Prepare for advanced airway if impending respiratory failure
• Have appropriate sized equipment ready
• ⚠ Intubation of children <12 requires Paramedic skill
12 EMT Transport Considerations
• Transport to pediatric-capable facility
• Alert receiving facility early
• Consider ALS intercept if BLS only
• Rapid transport if severe obstruction
