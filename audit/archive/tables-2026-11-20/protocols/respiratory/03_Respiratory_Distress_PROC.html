# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact Protocol:
• Assess airway patency and breathing adequacy
2 EMR Airway and Lung Assessment:
• Focused assessment for stridor, wheezing, rales
• Auscultate breath sounds bilaterally
• Assess work of breathing and accessory muscle use
3 EMT Continuous Monitoring:
• Continuous EtCO2 monitoring to guide therapy
• Continuous pulse oximetry
3a I • Cardiac monitoring
4 EMR Oxygen Administration (if SpO2 <94%):
• Supplemental Oxygen titrated to maintain SpO2 >94%
• Start with nasal cannula or non-rebreather as needed
• Avoid excessive FiO2 in COPD patients (target 88-92%)
5 EMR Positioning:
• Patient position of comfort (sitting upright preferred)
• Consider reverse Trendelenburg if tolerated
6 EMT CPAP (Continuous Positive Airway Pressure):
• Indications: Adults with respiratory distress due to pulmonary edema, infection, or near-drowning
• Initial Settings: PEEP 5 cmH2O
• Close monitoring for hypotension or pneumothorax
⚠ See Pearls for all contraindications
6a I • Titration: Titrate PEEP up to 10 cmH2O to support ventilation and work of breathing.
7 EMT Supraglottic Airways (SGA):
• Consider if patient continues to deteriorate and cannot tolerate CPAP
• Maintains airway patency while avoiding invasive procedures
8 I Advanced Airway Management (ETI):
• Endotracheal intubation reserved for I-level providers per protocol
9 A IV/IO Access:
• Establish vascular access for medication administration and fluid therapy
• IO if IV unobtainable
10 A Fluid Administration:
• 0.9% Sodium Chloride or appropriate fluid as clinically indicated
• Monitor for signs of fluid overload in CHF patients
11 A Stridor Management (Upper Airway):
• Epinephrine 3 mg of 1 mg/mL (1:1,000) nebulized
• May be repeated once
• May need assistance to hold nebulizer
• Administer simultaneously with the nebulized Epinephrine treatment:
• Methylprednisolone 125 mg IV/IO (adult) or 2 mg/kg IV/IO pediatric (maximum 125 mg)
⚠ Single dose only
12 A Bronchiolitis (Pediatric ONLY):
• 0.9% Sodium Chloride 6 mL nebulized
• Repeat as needed for increased work of breathing
• Transport in position of comfort with caregiver
13 A Pulmonary Edema:
• If Dyspnea with crackles and/or jugular venous distention:
• Nitroglycerin 0.4 mg sublingual (adult only)
• Repeat every 5 minutes up to 3 doses if SBP >100 mmHg (MAP >65 mmHg)
• Consider CPAP if available and no contraindications
14 I Severe Asthma/COPD:
• Refer to Asthma specific protocol for comprehensive treatment
• Magnesium Sulfate per Asthma protocol
⚠ Positive pressure may worsen pneumothorax or cause breath stacking
15 EMT Cardiac Ischemia Management:
• Evaluate for cardiac origin
• Consider 12-lead ECG
• Refer to Chest Pain/ACS protocol if indicated
16 EMT Respiratory Failure Management:
• If apnea/agonal respirations or GCS ≤13:
• Prepare for immediate airway control
• Bag-valve-mask ventilation
• Transport without delay
16a I • Consider advanced airway per provider level — patients 12 years and older
16b P • Consider advanced airway per provider level — patients under 12 years of age
