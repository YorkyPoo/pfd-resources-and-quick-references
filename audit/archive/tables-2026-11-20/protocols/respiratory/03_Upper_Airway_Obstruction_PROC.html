# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Patient Care:
• Apply to all patients per protocol.
3 EMT Conscious Adult (≥12 years) - Abdominal Thrusts:
• Stand behind patient, wrap arms around waist.
• Make fist with one hand; thumb side against patient's abdomen.
• Position fist midline, below xiphoid process and above navel.
• Grasp fist with other hand and deliver quick, upward thrusts.
• Repeat until obstruction clears or patient becomes unconscious.
4 EMT Conscious Obese Adult / Pregnant Woman - Chest Thrusts:
• Stand behind patient, arms under patient's armpits.
• Make fist with one hand; thumb side against sternum.
• Position fist midline on lower half of sternum.
• Grasp fist with other hand and deliver backward thrusts.
• Repeat until obstruction clears or patient becomes unconscious.
5 EMT Conscious Pediatric ≥1 year - Abdominal Thrusts:
• Kneel behind child, wrap arms around waist.
• Make fist; thumb side against child's abdomen.
• Position fist midline, below xiphoid process and above navel.
• Deliver quick, upward thrusts until obstruction clears.
6 EMT Conscious Pediatric <1 year - Back Blows and Chest Thrusts:
• Position infant prone, head down, below level of chest.
• Support head and neck with hand; jaw supported by thumb and fingers.
• Deliver 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades using heel of hand.
• Turn infant supine, head down; deliver 5 chest thrusts on lower sternum.
• Repeat alternating 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts until obstruction clears.
7 EMT Unconscious Patient - Position and Open Airway:
• Place patient supine on firm surface.
• Open airway using head-tilt chin-lift or jaw-thrust if trauma suspected.
8 EMT Unconscious Patient - BLS Airway Adjuncts:
• Insert OPA or NPA as indicated by patient tolerance and level of consciousness.
• Insert SGA (i-gel or King) if unable to maintain airway with basic maneuvers.
9 EMR Suctioning:
• Provide oropharyngeal suctioning as needed for secretions, blood, or vomit.
• Use rigid Yankauer catheter for direct visualization.
10 EMT Apneic Patient - BVM Ventilation:
• Support respirations with BVM and appropriate airway adjunct.
• Ensure adequate seal and proper ventilation rate.
• Monitor SpO2 and EtCO2 continuously.
11 I Unable to Ventilate - Laryngoscopy and Forceps Removal:
• If unable to ventilate due to foreign body obstruction, perform direct laryngoscopy.
• Visualize foreign body using laryngoscope blade.
• Remove obstruction with Magill forceps under direct visualization.
• Do NOT perform blind finger sweeps (may impact object deeper).
12 I Unable to Ventilate - Endotracheal Intubation:
• If apneic and unable to ventilate by less invasive means, consider endotracheal intubation.
• Visualize cords and pass ET tube under direct visualization.
⚠ Intubation of children <12 years is a Paramedic "red dot" skill only.
13 I+MC Post-Obstruction Advanced Airway - Sedation:
• If endotracheal intubation performed, refer to Sedation Post Advanced Airway protocol.
• Fentanyl (25 mcg) and Midazolam (2.5 mg) per Respiratory Arrest protocol.
• Use half-doses in elderly or frail patients.
14 P Expanding Hematoma / Edema - Advanced Airway:
• Early airway intervention before complete obstruction.
• Consider difficult airway algorithm; prepare surgical airway backup.
• Contact Medical Control for guidance in complex airways.
