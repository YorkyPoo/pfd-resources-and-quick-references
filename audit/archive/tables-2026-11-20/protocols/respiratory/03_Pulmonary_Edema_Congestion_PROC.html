# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol
2 EMR Perform focused evaluation including assessment of airway and lung sounds
3 EMR Provide supplemental oxygen to maintain SpO2 > 94%
4 EMT Consider non-invasive positive pressure ventilation (CPAP) for severe respiratory distress
5 EMT CPAP Initial Settings: PEEP 5 cmH2O
9 I CPAP Titration: Adjust PEEP up to 10 cmH2O to support ventilation and work of breathing
10 A Nitroglycerin
Adult: 0.4 mg SL
11 A Nitroglycerin Repeat Administration:
May repeat every 5 minutes as needed if SBP remains > 100 mmHg / MAP > 65 mmHg (maximum 3 doses)
12 I Furosemide (Lasix)
Adult: 40 mg IV/IO slow push
13 MC Contact Medical Control for guidance as needed
