# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Assess and document psychological characteristics (passivity, fear, aggression, etc.).
2 POLICY Assess for physical signs or injuries that are inconsistent with the reported mechanism of injury (MOI).
3 POLICY Assess for signs of neglect (hygiene, clothing, malnutrition, absence of caregivers).
4 POLICY Report Elder Abuse: Notify local DSS or Adult Protective Services (APS). If imminent danger of death/serious injury or death occurred, report to medical examiner and law enforcement.
5 POLICY Report Child Abuse: Notify local DSS or Child Protective Services (CPS). If transporting, it is acceptable to report to the attending ED physician.
6 POLICY Report Hunting Injuries: Notify Dept of Game and Inland Fisheries (DGIF) for serious bodily injury or death.
7 POLICY Document all notifications (e.g., "made notification to Dr. [ ]", hotline calls) thoroughly on the PCR.
