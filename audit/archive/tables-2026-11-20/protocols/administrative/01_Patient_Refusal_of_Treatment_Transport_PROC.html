# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Conduct a thorough assessment of the patient including vital signs and physical exam in accordance with relevant patient care protocols.
2 POLICY Assess right/capacity to refuse (≥ 14 years of age, fully oriented, understands risks/benefits/alternatives, no risk to self/others).
3 POLICY Identify appropriate surrogate decision maker (in descending order) if the patient lacks capacity or competence.
4 POLICY Clearly document information provided; obtain signatures for refusal from patient or surrogate and at least 1 adult witness.
5 POLICY Contact law enforcement regarding issuance of a Psychiatric ECO for patients with acute psychiatric emergencies risk of harm.
6 POLICY Contact Medical Control regarding issuance of a Medical ECO for acute medical emergencies lacking capacity.
7 POLICY Contact Medical Control for assistance if unsure of capacity, surrogate validity, or if patient refuses despite severe risk.
