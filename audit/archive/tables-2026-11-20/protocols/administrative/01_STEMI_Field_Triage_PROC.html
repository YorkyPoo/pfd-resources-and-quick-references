# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Acquire 12-Lead ECG
Must be completed within 5 minutes of patient arrival.
2 POLICY ECG Interpretation
Identify STEMI based on cardiac symptoms AND 1mm+ ST elevation in 2+ contiguous leads.
3 POLICY Routing - PCI Capable Hospital
Transport to the closest PCI capable facility if EMS ground transport time is < 30 minutes.
4 POLICY Routing - HEMS Consideration
If ground transport to a PCI facility is > 30 minutes, evaluate Helicopter EMS (HEMS) transport within 30 minutes.
5 POLICY Routing - Closest ED
Transport to the closest ED (regardless of PCI capability) if the patient has a compromised airway or impending circulatory collapse.
6 POLICY Notifications
Activate STEMI alert system to closest PCI center within 30 minutes. Call COR with "Pre-Hospital STEMI Alert" and patient Name/DOB. Transmit ECG and provide printed/electronic copy to facility/HEMS.
7 MC Serial ECGs
For atypical/indeterminate ECGs with high suspicion, transmit ECG, contact Medical Control, and obtain serial ECGs every 10 minutes.
