# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Request HEMS: Request air ambulance standby/response according to department procedure (no medical control authorization needed).
2 POLICY Establish Landing Zone: Establish a safe and logistically feasible landing zone.
3 POLICY Provide Patient Info: Establish number of patients, primary injuries/medical condition, and approximate patient weight(s). Forward this information to the responding air medical service.
4 POLICY Prepare Patient: Trauma patients should be fully immobilized. IV/IO access should be established as time allows (within provider scope).
5 POLICY Designate Reporter: On scene, designate one individual who will be responsible for providing the patient report(s) to the flight crew upon arrival.
6 POLICY Provide Documentation: Ensure PCR is provided to receiving facility as per 12 VAC 5-31.
