# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Comprehensive Assessment: Complete pertinent patient history, physical assessment, and full set of vital signs.
2 POLICY Clinical Verification: Confirm ALS care is not currently medically necessary and will likely not be necessary in the near future (to include pain management).
3 POLICY BLS Verification: Verify BLS provider is willing to accept responsibility and capable of providing a level of care that meets the assessed and documented needs.
4 POLICY Transfer of Care: Transfer Attendant in Charge (AIC) responsibilities to the BLS provider.
5 POLICY Medical Control: Contact Medical Control when there is a concern of the appropriateness of the release.
6 POLICY BLS Documentation: Complete a Patient Care Report (PCR).
7 POLICY ALS Documentation: Complete documentation in, or as an addendum to, the PCR (physical findings, transfer of care, physician consultation).
8 POLICY QA/QI Review: Any ALS Release to BLS must be reviewed by the Officer or Acting Officer the following day, per departmental SOP.
