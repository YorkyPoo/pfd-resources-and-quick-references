# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Transport to patient choice: Transport capable patients to the ED of their choice (if no exceptions)
2 POLICY Reroute for STEMI criteria: Transport to STEMI Receiving Center per the Regional STEMI Plan
3 POLICY Reroute for Stroke criteria: Transport to designated stroke center per the Regional Stroke Triage Plan
4 POLICY Transport to closest: Transport to the closest most appropriate hospital if no patient preference
5 POLICY Document reason: Document reason for any reroute or destination change on the PCR
6 POLICY Contact HEMS: Contact HEMS without Medical Control if beneficial to the patient
7 POLICY Initiate ground transport: Initiate ground transport to closest hospital/helispot if HEMS > 10 min delay
