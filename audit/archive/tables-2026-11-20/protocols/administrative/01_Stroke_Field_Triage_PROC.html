# Basis Action / Intervention
1 EMT Time Management: Maintain a goal scene time of less than 15 minutes.
2 POLICY Diagnostics: Assess blood glucose. If < 60 mg/dL, treat per Hypoglycemia protocol.
3 POLICY Neurological Assessment: Evaluate patient using BEFAST criteria and RACE scale.
5 POLICY Routing (< 24 Hours & LVO): If Symptoms < 24 Hours (or unknown) AND Suspected LVO (RACE Score 5-9), transport to closest Comprehensive Stroke Center IF it does not increase transport time by 30 mins or greater. If it does, transport to nearest Primary Stroke Center.
6 POLICY Routing (> 24 Hours OR No LVO): If Symptoms > 24 Hours OR Low Suspicion for LVO (RACE Score 0-4), transport to closest Stroke Center (Acute Stroke Ready, Primary, or Comprehensive).
7 POLICY Stroke Alert Activation: If RACE ≥ 1, activate stroke alert system by notifying appropriate facility early.
8 POLICY Communication: Early notification to Medical Control and/or Stroke Center. During transport, call COR with Name and DOB for rapid registration.
