# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Use HEAR for notification only
Notify hospitals of BLS/ALS patients NOT requiring on-line physician intervention.
2 POLICY Use COR for medical control/meds/alerts
Direct contact with Medical Control for physician consultation. Used for Stroke, Trauma, STEMI alerts (providing Name/DOB).
3 POLICY Transmit standard pre-arrival report
Include Unit ID/ETA, patient age/gender/chief complaint, history of present illness/injury, pertinent medical history, significant physical findings, vital signs, and treatment.
4 POLICY MCI comms suspension/supervisor relay
Routine ambulance-to-ED communications suspended. Transport group supervisor communicates directly to coordinating ED.
5 POLICY Comm Failure: act on standing orders
Follow the appropriate Patient Care Protocols based on clinical situation. All orders within scope of practice become standing orders.
6 POLICY Document comm failure
Document time, nature of comms problem, clinical status, and protocols used on the PCR. Review with Officer/Acting Officer per department SOPs.
