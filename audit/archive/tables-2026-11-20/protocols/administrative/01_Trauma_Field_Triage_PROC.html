# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Scene Time Management: Maintain a goal scene time of < 10 minutes.
2 POLICY Immediate Life-Threats Routing: If unmanageable airway, uncontrolled hemorrhage, or traumatic cardiac arrest, transport immediately to closest ED.
3 POLICY Physiologic Criteria Met Routing: Transport to closest appropriate trauma center (Level I or II) within 30-min ground transport.
4 POLICY Out of Range (Ground) / HEMS Consideration: If not within 30-min ground transport, transport to closest hospital UNLESS Helicopter EMS (HEMS) can deliver to trauma center faster.
5 POLICY Mechanism-Only Routing: If patient meets mechanism criteria but NOT physiologic/anatomic criteria, contact Medical Control to determine destination.
6 POLICY Burn Center Routing: For burns meeting criteria absent other trauma, transport directly to Burn Center.
7 POLICY Pediatric Trauma Routing: Transport severely injured pediatric patients to a pediatric trauma center (CHKD or VCU).
8 POLICY Trauma Alerts: Establish early radio contact with the receiving trauma center.
