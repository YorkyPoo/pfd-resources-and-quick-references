# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Request Law Enforcement if needed
Call for assistance prior to attempting restraint procedures.
2 POLICY Rule out/treat medical causes
Consider and treat medical causes of combativeness (hypoxia, head injury, hypoglycemia).
3 POLICY Place patient supine on backboard
NEVER PRONE.
4 POLICY Secure extremities/check circulation every 15 mins
Attempt lower extremities first (soft restraints around both ankles), then arms at sides. Secure backboard to stretcher. If necessary, utilize cervical spine precautions.
5 POLICY Apply SpO2 and monitor respiratory status
Evaluate respiratory/cardiac status to assure no airway compromise exists; apply supplemental oxygen if indicated.
6 POLICY Avoid impeding respiratory function with chest straps
DO NOT tighten chest straps to the point of impeding respiratory function.
7 POLICY Chemical Restraint: Assess vitals <5 mins then q10 mins
And prior to any repeat dose. Monitor ECG, obtain 12-Lead, consider IN medications and establish IV/IO if possible.
8 POLICY Refer to Behavioral Emergencies Protocol for sedatives
Sedative agents may be needed to restrain violently combative patients. (Refer to the Behavioral Emergencies Protocol).
