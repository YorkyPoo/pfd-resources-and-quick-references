# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Secure Equipment: Secure all monitoring devices and other equipment to prevent injury.
2 POLICY Safe Driving: Drive cautiously and at safe speeds, observing applicable traffic laws.
3 POLICY Child Restraint: Ensure pediatric patients < 40 kg (88 lbs) are restrained with approved child restraint devices secured per the manufacturer’s instructions.
4 POLICY Provider Safety: Ensure that all EMS providers use available restraining devices or systems during transport.
5 POLICY Caregiver Safety: Secure parents or caregivers using safety belts.
6 POLICY Alternative Transport: Attempt to arrange for transportation of adults and children who are not patients by alternative means.
7 POLICY Comfort Measures: For pediatric patients having medical conditions aggravated by stress, make every reasonable attempt to maximize safety while comforting the child.
