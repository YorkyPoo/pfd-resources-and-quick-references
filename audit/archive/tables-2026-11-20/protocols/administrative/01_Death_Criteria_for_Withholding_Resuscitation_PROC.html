# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Withhold CPR for obvious mortal injury, rigor mortis, dependent lividity, decomposition, or valid DDNR.
2 POLICY Initiate CPR if doubt exists regarding the DNR validity, or if only a living will/advanced directive is present.
3 POLICY Provide full BLS/ALS care prior to termination (BLS: AED, pulse ox, EtCO2, supraglottic airway; ALS: advanced airway, IV/IO, meds per ACLS).
4 POLICY Contact Medical Control for termination decision or DNR questions. Provide thorough report of history, condition, and treatment.
5 POLICY Remain on scene and notify law enforcement and Medical Examiner if terminated on scene. Support bystanders and family.
