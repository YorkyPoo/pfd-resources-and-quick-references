# Basis Action / Intervention
1 POLICY Assess decision-making capacity
2 POLICY Direct Transport: Once identified, consider direct transport to a Mental Health Facility.
3 POLICY Medical Control: When in doubt regarding appropriate destination or patient status, contact Medical Control.
4 POLICY Psychiatric Emergencies: Acute psychiatric emergencies with significant risk of harm to self/others refusing treatment/transport: contact law enforcement regarding Emergency Custody Order (ECO) for psychiatric evaluation.
5 POLICY Medical Emergencies: Acute medical emergencies lacking decision-making capacity refusing treatment/transport: contact Medical Control regarding ECO for medical evaluation. MC must petition a magistrate. Contact law enforcement to assist.
