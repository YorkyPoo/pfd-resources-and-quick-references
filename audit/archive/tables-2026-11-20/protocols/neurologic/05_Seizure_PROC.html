# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Prevent injury (DO NOT restrain while seizing)
2 EMR Provide supplemental O2/protect airway
3 EMT Assess blood glucose (Treat per protocol)
4 EMR Refer to Overdose/Poisoning protocol if indicated
5 A* Actively seizing >5 mins:
Midazolam 5 mg IV/IO or 10 mg IM (Adult & Ped >40kg)
\
6 A* Use HALF dose Midazolam for frail/hypotensive/elderly >65y
7 MC Contact Medical Control and initiate transport if seizing >5 mins after 2nd dose of Midazolam
