# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Universal Care / Assess BEFAST
2 EMR Assess RACE scale for Large Vessel Occlusion (LVO)
3 EMR Record Time Last Seen Normal and family contact info
4 EMT Assess blood glucose (Treat <60 per Hypoglycemia protocol)
5 EMT Goal scene time <15 minutes / Initiate rapid transport
6 EMT Triage: Symptoms <24 hrs & RACE 5-9 -> Transport to closest Comprehensive Stroke Center (if <30 min delay)
7 EMT Triage: Symptoms >24 hrs OR RACE 0-4 -> Transport to closest stroke center
8 PENDING Establish 18g IV in Right AC (preferred for rapid imaging)
9 PENDING Call COR at the receiving facility with Name/DOB for rapid registration
