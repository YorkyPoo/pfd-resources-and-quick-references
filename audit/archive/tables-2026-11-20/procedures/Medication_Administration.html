# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMT Prepare medication. Refer to protocol-specific indications and pharmacology section.
2 EMT Cross-check: Verify correct medication, patient, route, dose, and expiration with another provider.
3 A IV Administration: Verify patency, administer at prescribed rate (Push=Rapid; Slow Push=30-60s; Infusion=per rate).
4 A IO Administration: Verify patency, follow IV administration steps.
5 EMT IN Administration: Administer ½ dose per naris using MAD. Max 1mL/naris.
6 EMT IM Administration: Clean site, insert at 90° angle, administer, dispose in sharps.
