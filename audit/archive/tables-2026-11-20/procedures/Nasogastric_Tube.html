# Level Action / Intervention
1 I Measure length: Nose to ear to xiphoid process.
2 I Lubricate tip with Lidocaine gel and pass through nostril posteriorly along the floor of the nasal passage.
3 I Advance gently until measured length reached. Ensure not coiled in oropharynx.
4 I Confirm placement: Inject 20cc air and auscultate over epigastrium for bubbles.
5 I Secure to nose and attach to low/medium suction.
6 I Document indication, insertion depth, and output volume.
