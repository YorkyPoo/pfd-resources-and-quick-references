# Level Action / Intervention
1 I Expose/clean chest, apply 4-lead ECG.
2 I Apply pads (anterior/posterior) and set to pacing mode.
3 I Set rate at 80 bpm. Initial current 50mA.
4 I Increase current (10mA/10s) until electrical capture achieved.
5 I Palpate femoral pulse to confirm mechanical capture.
