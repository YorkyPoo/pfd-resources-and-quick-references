# Level Action / Intervention
1 A Locate site: Proximal humerus (greater tubercle) or Proximal tibia (2-3 finger breadths below patella, 2 finger breadths medial).
2 A Clean site with alcohol/disinfectant.
3 A Insert needle perpendicular until tip touches bone (confirm 1 black line visible).
4 A Drill until loss of resistance is felt (medullary space entry).
5 A Remove driver, remove stylet (turn counter-clockwise), dispose in sharps.
6 A Secure needle, attach primed extension tubing.
7 A Confirm/Flush: Aspirate to confirm (if unconscious), flush with 10–20 mL 0.9% Sodium Chloride.
8 A Pain Control (Conscious Patient): Administer 2% Lidocaine slow push (Adult: 1mg/kg max 40mg; Ped: 0.5mg/kg max 20mg). Dwell 1 min, flush.
