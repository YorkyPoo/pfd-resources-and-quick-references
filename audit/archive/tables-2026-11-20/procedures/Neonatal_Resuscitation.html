# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Warm, dry, stimulate. Position airway (sniffing). Clear secretions ONLY if obstructed or thick meconium.
2 EMR If apnea/gasping/HR <100: BVM with 21% O₂ for 30s, then 100% O₂. Rate: 40-60/min.
3 I Apply ECG and EtCO₂ monitoring.
4 EMR If HR <60/min or pulseless: Begin compressions (3:1 ratio, 30 breaths/min).
5 A Obtain IV/IO access
5a I Administer Epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mg/mL). Repeat q3-5 mins.
