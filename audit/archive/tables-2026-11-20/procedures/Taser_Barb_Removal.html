# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMT Coordinate with law enforcement (scene/patient safe).
2 EMT Ensure Taser cartridge removed/wires cut.
3 EMT Assess for injuries (burns, electrocution, trauma).
4 EMT Leave in place and transport if in: Eye, face, neck, breast, axilla, genitals, hands, or feet.
5 EMT Hold skin taut. Grasp electrode shaft with hemostat/forceps. Pull straight out with quick motion.
6 EMT Dispose in sharps container, provide wound care.
