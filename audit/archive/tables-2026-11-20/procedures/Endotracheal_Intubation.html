# Level Action / Intervention
0 EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol.
1 EMT Pre-oxygenate: 100% O₂ via NRB or BVM for 3 mins. Monitor SpO₂.
2 EMT Position patient: Ear to sternal notch. Maintain in-line C-spine stabilization if trauma suspected.
3 I Prepare equipment: ETT (appropriate size + 1 size below), stylet/bougie, O₂, suction, laryngoscope, backup airway (SGA), BVM with EtCO₂.
4 I Orally intubate (Patients ≥ 12 years).
5 P Orally intubate (Patients < 12 years).
6 I If unsuccessful, re-oxygenate with 100% O₂. After 2 failed attempts, manage via most experienced provider or alternative airway.
7 P If unable to intubate and unable to oxygenate, proceed immediately to Surgical Airway.
8 I Verify placement: Continuous box-waveform EtCO₂, bilateral lung sounds, absence of gastric sounds.
9 I Secure tube: Tape or commercial holder. Document depth.
10 I Administer analgesia/sedation as needed.
11 EMT Monitor ET tube placement and ventilation with SpO₂ and continuous box-waveform EtCO₂.
