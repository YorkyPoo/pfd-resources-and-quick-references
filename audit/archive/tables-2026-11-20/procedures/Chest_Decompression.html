# Level Action / Intervention
1 I Administer oxygen at 15 LPM (100% FiO2).
2 I Locate 2nd intercostal space in the mid-clavicular line on the side of suspected pneumothorax (Alternate: 4th intercostal space, mid-axillary line).
3 I Clean the site.
4 I Attach a 10 mL syringe filled with 0.9% Sodium Chloride to a 14-gauge catheter needle (longest available).
5 I Aspirate the syringe while advancing over the rib, perpendicular to the skin, into the pleural space until a pop is felt or air/blood/fluid is freely aspirated. Do not advance needle further.
6 I Advance the catheter over the needle into the pleural space and remove the needle.
7 I Reassess the patient, including vitals and respiratory effort/compliance. Consider repeating for continued/recurrent instability.
