# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMT Explain procedure and indications to the patient.
2 EMT Ensure adequate oxygen supply to the CPAP device.
3 EMT Set positive end-expiratory pressure (PEEP) to 5 cmH2O.
4 EMT Place mask over mouth/nose with oxygen flowing. Secure with straps, starting with lower straps. Minimize air leak.
5 EMT Reassess patient (SpO2, EtCO2, work of breathing).
6 I Consider titrating PEEP (5–15 mmHg) as needed to improve ventilation.
