# Level Action / Intervention
1 P Position supine, expose/clean neck, palpate cricothyroid membrane.
2 P Surgical Cric: Vertical incision (2-3cm), horizontal incision through membrane, insert bougie, pass ETT (size 4.0-6.0).
3 P Needle Cric: 14g catheter with syringe. Aspirate while advancing until pop/air aspiration. Advance catheter, remove needle.
4 P Confirm/monitor placement with EtCO₂ and SpO₂.
5 MC Contact Medical Control and transport to closest ED.
