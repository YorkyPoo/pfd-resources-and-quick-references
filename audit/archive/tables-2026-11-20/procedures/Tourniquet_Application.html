# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMR Expose injury, assess for additional trauma.
2 EMR Place proximal to injury (not over joint/fracture).
3 EMR Tighten until bleeding stops and distal pulse is absent.
4 EMR If bleeding continues, apply 2nd tourniquet proximal and adjacent.
5 EMR Indicate time/date of application; keep exposed/visible.
6 EMR DO NOT remove/loosen once applied. Consider pain control.
