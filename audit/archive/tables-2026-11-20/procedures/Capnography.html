# Level Action / Intervention
1 EMT Adequate respiratory effort: Apply EtCO₂ sampling nasal cannula to patient.
2 EMT Inadequate respiratory effort / Apnea: Attach in-line EtCO₂ sampling device to BVM, CPAP, or ventilator.
3 EMT Continuous Monitoring: Monitor reading and waveform continuously.
4 EMT Advanced Airway (ETT/SGA): Continuous monitoring is mandatory. Sudden drop or loss of EtCO₂ signal requires immediate verification of airway device placement and ventilation.
5 EMT Cardiac Arrest: If EtCO₂ < 10 mmHg or dropping during CPR, optimize compression rate, depth, and recoil. If ROSC is achieved, adjust ventilation to maintain EtCO₂ 35-45 mmHg. Do not hyperventilate.
6 EMT TBI / Stroke: Adjust ventilation to maintain EtCO₂ 35-45 mmHg. Do not hyperventilate.
7 EMT Obstructive airway disease (Asthma/COPD): Consider permissive hypercapnia. Prioritize oxygenation.
