# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Procedures - Vaccination Administration

### Overview
Guidelines for vaccination administration by authorized, appropriately trained, and supervised EMS providers for public health.

### Responsibilities
*   **OMD:** Manages vaccine handling/training, acts as supervising physician, implements quality management.
*   **Agency:** Must be a licensed ALS agency, establish MOU/registration for immunization programs, and maintain records per VIIS guidelines.
*   **Provider:** Must hold valid, unrestricted ALS certification and be individually approved by the OMD.

### Procedure
1. Don PPE per CDC/agency guidelines.
2. Ensure patient meets vaccine criteria and obtain informed consent.
3. Screen for contraindications/precautions (if met, do not administer).
4. Explain procedure and provide vaccine information statement.
5. Administer per manufacturer guidelines.
6. Observe for 15 minutes for adverse reactions.
7. Report adverse reactions to VAERS and document in VIIS.

### Pearls
*   Vaccines under Emergency Use Authorization (EUA) require recipients receive a specific EUA Fact Sheet.
*   Refer to manufacturer guidelines for specific contraindications and precautions.
*   If adverse effects occur, refer to the appropriate PEMS Patient Care Protocol.
