# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Procedures - Nasogastric Tube Insertion

### Overview
Used for gastric decompression in intubated patients, airway management in patients with massive hematemesis, or persistent large volume emesis.

### Procedure
<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Measure length: Nose to ear to xiphoid process. |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Lubricate tip with [Lidocaine](../pharmacology/Lidocaine.md) gel and pass through nostril posteriorly along the floor of the nasal passage. |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Advance gently until measured length reached. Ensure not coiled in oropharynx. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | **Confirm placement:** Inject 20cc air and auscultate over epigastrium for bubbles. |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Secure to nose and attach to low/medium suction. |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Document indication, insertion depth, and output volume. |

</div>

### Pearls
*   Contraindicated for patients with known esophageal varices (without active hemorrhage), recent maxillofacial surgery, or acute maxillofacial trauma.
*   In conscious patients, swallowing a sip of water may facilitate placement.
*   For massive GI bleeds, consider placement prior to intubation to improve airway visualization.
