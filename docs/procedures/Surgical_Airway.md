# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Procedures - Surgical Airway

### Overview
Last-resort airway management for patients who cannot be oxygenated or intubated by less invasive means.

### Indications
- Inability to oxygenate by less invasive means.
- **Surgical Cricothyrotomy:** ≥ 12 years old or > 35-40 kg.
- **Needle Cricothyrotomy:** < 12 years old or < 35-40 kg.

### Procedure
<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-p">P</span> | Position supine, expose/clean neck, palpate cricothyroid membrane. |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-p">P</span> | **Surgical Cric:** Vertical incision (2-3cm), horizontal incision through membrane, insert bougie, pass ETT (size 4.0-6.0). |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-p">P</span> | **Needle Cric:** 14g catheter with syringe. Aspirate while advancing until pop/air aspiration. Advance catheter, remove needle. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-p">P</span> | Confirm/monitor placement with EtCO₂ and SpO₂. |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-mc">MC</span> | Contact Medical Control and transport to closest ED. |

</div>

### Pearls
*   Needle cricothyrotomy is unlikely to provide adequate ventilation. Prioritize oxygenation and transport.
*   Clearly document attempts at less invasive airway management prior to surgical airway.
*   Bleeding is common; utilize in-line suctioning.
*   If unable to secure airway, contact Medical Control and expedite transport.
