# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Procedures - Neonatal Resuscitation

### Overview
Resuscitation protocol for newborns/neonates (<28 days) with heart rate <100/min, apnea, or lack of pulse.

### Procedure
<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Warm, dry, stimulate. Position airway (sniffing). Clear secretions ONLY if obstructed or thick meconium. |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | If apnea/gasping/HR <100: BVM with 21% O₂ for 30s, then 100% O₂. Rate: 40-60/min. |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Apply ECG and EtCO₂ monitoring. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | If HR <60/min or pulseless: Begin compressions (3:1 ratio, 30 breaths/min). |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-p">P</span><sup class="lvl-own" title="OWNER_REQUIRED: no PEMS 2024 row authorises this action's provider level">&#9873;</sup> | Obtain IV/IO access, administer [Epinephrine](../pharmacology/Epinephrine.md) 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mg/mL). Repeat q3-5 mins. |

</div>

<p class="lv-note">&#9873; <strong>OWNER_REQUIRED</strong> — row 5: this row prints two actions that PEMS 2024 splits across two rows at two lower levels — "Obtain IV/IO access if not already obtained." (p.261#10, badge A) and "Administer: Epinephrine (Adrenaline) 0.01mg/kg (0.1mg/mL) IV/IO" (p.261#12, badge I). No p.261 row prints either at P, so the P this page publishes has no PEMS authorisation and stays flagged. See <code>audit/level-scan/level-rule-ledger.md</code>.</p>

### Pearls
*   Routine suctioning is no longer recommended.
*   Palpating a pulse on a neonate can be difficult; palpate at the umbilical stump or use a stethoscope on the chest.
*   Use a 3-way stopcock for precise fluid amounts. Do not use pressure bags.
*   Neonates are at high risk for hypoglycemia; assess glucose <40 mg/dL (newborn) or <60 mg/dL (neonate) and treat with D10W 5mL/kg.
