# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Procedures - Tourniquet Application

### Overview
Management of life-threatening extremity hemorrhage uncontrolled by direct pressure or wound packing.

### Procedure
<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Expose injury, assess for additional trauma. |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Place proximal to injury (not over joint/fracture). |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Tighten until bleeding stops and distal pulse is absent. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | If bleeding continues, apply 2nd tourniquet proximal and adjacent. |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Indicate time/date of application; keep exposed/visible. |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | DO NOT remove/loosen once applied. Consider pain control. |

</div>

### Pearls
*   Commercial device is preferred; BP cuff is an acceptable alternative; improvised tourniquets are unreliable.
*   For crush/suspension injuries, consider pre-positioning tourniquets prior to extrication.
*   Do not release tourniquet once applied.
*   Document location and time of application for receiving staff.
