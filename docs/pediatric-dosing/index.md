# Poquoson Pediatric Dosing Reference

!!! danger "OMD Review Required"
    This pediatric dosing reference is a **calculational aid only**. All doses must be verified against current Poquoson Fire & Rescue Patient Care Protocols and approved by the Operational Medical Director (OMD) before clinical use. In the event of any discrepancy between this reference and the published protocols, the published protocols take precedence.


A **length-based pediatric dosing system** for rapid, accurate medication dosing, equipment sizing, and defibrillation/cardioversion energy selection. All doses below are derived from **Poquoson Fire & Rescue Patient Care Protocols**.

---

## How Length-Based Dosing Works

In pediatric emergencies, you may not have access to a scale. **Patient length is a reliable proxy for weight** — the relationship between height and weight in children follows predictable growth curves established in pediatric emergency medicine. By estimating length, you can determine the weight zone and read pre-calculated doses directly, eliminating the need for on-scene math.

!!! tip "Estimation Method"
    To estimate length: lay the patient supine and place your hand(s) alongside the body from crown to heel. Compare to the hand breadth estimates above. The hand measurement is approximate — use the nearest zone.

!!! warning "Always Use Actual Weight When Available"
    Length-based estimates are for situations where you cannot obtain an accurate weight. When a reliable weight is known, use it directly with the [Drug Calculator](../medications.md).

## How to Use This Reference

1. **Estimate the patient's length** and find their zone in the table above
2. **Go to the [Pre-Calculated Dosing Tables](dosing-tables.md)** for that zone
3. **Read all doses** — medications, equipment sizes, and energy settings are pre-computed
4. **Refer to the specific protocol** for complete clinical decision-making
