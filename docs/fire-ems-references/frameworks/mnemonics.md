## Coal was Wealth
A critical 13-point [Fire Engineering](https://digital.fireengineering.com/fireengineering/201806/MobilePagedArticle.action?articleId=1397285) mnemonic used by firefighters to perform a comprehensive scene size-up, covering Construction, Occupancy, Area, Life hazards, Water supply, Auxiliary systems, Street conditions, Weather, Exposures, Apparatus/personnel, Location, Time, and Height. This tool ensures rapid, safe decision-making during incident management. 

**COAL WAS WEALTH Breakdown (Size-Up Factors):**

- **C**onstruction: Building type (e.g., wood frame, fire-resistive).
- **O**ccupancy: Residential, commercial, or industrial use.
- **A**rea: Size of the building and potential for fire spread.
- **L**ife Hazard: Risk to occupants and firefighters.
- **W**ater Supply: Location of hydrants and availability.
- **A**uxiliary Systems: Fire sprinklers or standpipes present.
- **S**treet Conditions: Access for apparatus.
- **W**eather: Wind, temperature, and snow influencing fire behavior.
- **E**xposures: Adjacent structures at risk.
- **A**pparatus/Personnel: Resources on scene.
- **L**ocation: Specific area of fire (e.g., floor, room).
- **T**ime: Time of day (affects occupancy).
- **H**eight: Number of stories.

## RECEO-VS (Life Safety)
Rescue, Exposure, Confinement, Extinguishment, Overhaul, Ventilation, Salvage

## SLICE-RS (Property Conservation)
Size-up, Locate the fire, Identify and control flow path, Cool the space from safest location, and Extinguish the fire. Supplementing these are Rescue and Salvage.

## IDEAL
• Identify arriving units
• Describe what you see
• Explain what you intend to do
• Assume Command
• Let incoming units know what you want them to do.

## 4 L's
- Life
- Location (of the fire)
- Layout (of the structure)
- Level (of smoke/fire)

## Can Report 
- **Conditions:** Location, smoke/heat, layout, and hazards.
- Actions: Tasks being performed (e.g., search, fire attack).
- Needs: Required resources, tools, or relief.
**Purpose:** It ensures clear, concise, and consistent communication during incidents, helping incident commanders adjust strategies if objectives cannot be met.
