### Initial Operations (SAWCS)

**S - Sizeup**

- **FPODP:** _Facts, Probabilities, Own Situation, Decision, Plan of Operation._ The classic step-by-step size-up model to formulate an initial strategy.
    
- **7-Sides:** Assessing all aspects of the fire area (Top, Bottom, Front, Back, Left, Right, and Interior) to predict spread.
    
- **Construction:** Identifying the building construction type (Type I-V) to anticipate fire behavior and collapse risks.
    
- **Lap:** Completing a 360-degree walk-around of the structure to view the entire incident.
    
- **IOCAN:** _Identify, Objectives, Conditions, Actions, Needs._ A standardized format for clear, concise radio reporting.
    

**A - Apparatus**

- **3-Sides:** Positioning the first-arriving apparatus to effectively view at least three sides of the building.
    
- **Hot Zone:** Ensuring unassigned apparatus stage outside the immediate hazard and collapse zones to prevent gridlock.
    

**W - Water Supply**

- **Who:** Identifying which specific unit is responsible for establishing the water supply.
    
- **Where:** The physical location of the water source (hydrant or draft site).
    
- **What:** The type of supply line or operation being utilized (e.g., LDH, relay pumping).
    
- **FDC:** Locating and supplying the Fire Department Connection to support building sprinkler or standpipe systems.
    

**C - Command**

- **Name:** Designating the geographical or functional name of the command (e.g., "Main Street Command").
    
- **ICP:** Establishing a fixed, visible location for the Incident Command Post.
    

**S - Strategy**

- **Offensive:** Committing personnel to an interior attack to aggressively extinguish the fire at the seat.
    
- **Combination:** A transitional attack, typically softening the target from the exterior before moving interior.
    
- **Defensive:** Withdrawing personnel and focusing on exterior streams to protect exposures when the structure is unsafe or lost.

### Tactical Priorities (RECEO)

**S - Safety**

- **R.I.C.:** Establishing a Rapid Intervention Crew dedicated strictly to firefighter rescue.
    
- **2-out:** Satisfying the OSHA "Two-In, Two-Out" rule, ensuring two firefighters are standing by outside before interior entry is made.
    
- **PAR:** _Personnel Accountability Report._ Conducting a roll call to confirm all members are accounted for and safe.
    
- **Utilities:** Securing the structure's gas, electric, and water to prevent secondary hazards.
    

**R - Rescue**

- **1st:** Primary Search. A rapid, thorough search targeting areas with the highest probability of victims.
    
- **2nd:** Secondary Search. A slower, meticulous search conducted after the fire is controlled.
    
- **"ALL CLEAR":** The critical benchmark broadcasted when searches are complete and no victims are found.
    
- **Medical T/T:** Establishing Medical Triage and Transport for any civilian or responder casualties.
    

**E - Exposures**

- **Interior:** Protecting unburned areas and limiting fire extension inside the primary structure.
    
- **Exterior:** Protecting adjacent buildings, vehicles, or property from radiant heat and fire spread.
    

**C - Confinement**

- **Location:** Identifying the specific choke points where the fire must be cut off and stopped.
    

**E - Extinguishment**

- **Size:** Selecting the appropriate hose line diameter (e.g., 1 ¾", 2 ½") based on the fire load.
    
- **# of Lines:** Tracking the total number of attack lines currently deployed and operating.
    

**O - Overhaul**

- **Who:** Assigning fresh crews to check for and extinguish hidden fire extension.
    
- **Where:** Designating the specific walls, ceilings, or voids requiring overhaul.

### Ventilation, Salvage, and Support (VSS)

**V - Ventilation**

- **Vert:** Vertical ventilation, typically involving cutting the roof to release heat and smoke.
    
- **Horz:** Horizontal ventilation, utilizing doors and windows.
    
- **PPV:** Positive Pressure Ventilation, using fans to force clear air into the structure and exhaust smoke.
    

**S - Salvage**

- **Who:** Assigning units to deploy tarps and channel water to protect property.
    
- **Where:** Identifying the primary locations where salvage is critical (often directly below the fire floor).
    
- **RP:** _Responsible Party._ Identifying and communicating with the property owner or occupant.
    

**S - Support**

- **Law:** Requesting Law Enforcement for traffic control, crowd control, or security.
    
- **Invest:** Calling Fire Investigators to determine the cause and origin of the incident.
    
- **PIO:** Assigning a Public Information Officer to manage media and public communications.
    
- **Safety:** Designating a dedicated Incident Safety Officer (ISO) to monitor scene hazards.
    
- **Red Cross:** Requesting assistance for displaced occupants.
    
- **Util:** Calling utility companies for specialized shutoffs at the grid level.
    
- **Board up:** Securing the compromised structure post-incident.
    
- **Rehab:** Establishing a designated rehabilitation area for firefighter medical monitoring, rest, and hydration.
    
- **Air:** Requesting a mobile air unit or cascade system to refill empty SCBA cylinders.
    
- **Hazmat:** Requesting specialized Hazardous Materials teams if toxic or unidentified substances are involved.
