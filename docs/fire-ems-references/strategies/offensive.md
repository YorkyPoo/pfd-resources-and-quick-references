# Offensive Strategy

## 💡 Core Idea
- An **offensive strategy** commits crews to an interior attack at the seat of the fire, based on a favorable risk/gain assessment and a viable rescue profile.

## Explanation
- **Used when:**
    - Conditions support interior operations.
    - A viable rescue profile exists.
    - Adequate staffing is available.
    - Building stability is reasonably assured.
- **Drivers:** A moderate to confirmed victim profile justified by SIGNAL raises the acceptable level of risk to firefighters.
- **Requires:** an established water supply, a coordinated vent/attack (flow path control), and RIT standing by.

## Examples
- **Single-family dwelling:** First engine stretches a line to the front door and advances while a primary search is conducted — the classic offensive posture.
- **Confirmed victim:** Bystander reports a trapped occupant; offensive strategy is warranted despite challenging conditions.
