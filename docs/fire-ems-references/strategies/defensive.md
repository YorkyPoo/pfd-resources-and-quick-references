# Defensive Strategy

## 💡 Core Idea
- A **defensive strategy** withdraws crews from the interior and focuses on exterior streams, exposure protection, and collapse-zone safety when the building or fire conditions are no longer survivable.

## Explanation
- **Used when:**
    - Collapse is imminent.
    - Fire conditions exceed available resources.
    - No survivable space exists.
    - Risk to personnel is unacceptable.
- **Guiding principle:** Risk nothing for lives or property already lost. A low victim profile mandates low risk.
- **Requires:** clear defensive boundaries, SCBA even in defensive positions, and accountability so crews do not re-enter offensive spaces.

## Examples
- **Fully involved vacant structure:** Low victim profile, heavy involvement — pull crews out and protect exposures.
- **Imminent collapse:** Unsupported roof or mansard failure indicators — transition to exterior master streams.
