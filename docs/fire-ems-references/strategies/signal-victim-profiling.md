# SIGNAL Victim Profiling

## Core Idea
- A rapid-fire mnemonic used during the initial size-up to collect critical data from bystanders, enabling a calculated Risk-Gain decision for rescue operations.

## Explanation
- **S**pecies: Humans? Pets? Livestock? (Determines priority)
- **I**ncapacity: Are they mobile? Trapped? Medical emergency?
- **G**ender: Helps identify the victim in descriptions.
- **N**umber: How many? (Resource deployment)
- **A**ge: Child? Elderly? (Physical capabilities)
- **L**ocation: Where are they? Which floor? Trapped in which room?
- **Application**: "Just ten seconds invested in getting a SIGNAL from a bystander will pay huge dividends."

## Examples
- **Residential Fire**: Officer asks a neighbor: "Who is inside?" Neighbor: "My 70-year-old mom (Age/Species), she uses a walker (Incapacity), and she's in the bedroom (Location)."
- **Offensive/Defensive Decision**: A **Confirmed** profile (SIGNAL obtained) often justifies an **Offensive Strategy** regardless of fire conditions, whereas a **Low** profile (vacant building) suggests **Defensive** tactics to preserve firefighter safety.
