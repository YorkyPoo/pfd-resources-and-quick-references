# Burn Surface Area Estimation

Two tools, one job: how much of the body is burned. Estimation is famously imprecise —
**over-estimating drives fluid overload**, under-estimating under-resuscitates. Estimate,
say it out loud, and let the receiving team re-measure.

!!! warning "Reference only"
    Percentages below are the standard teaching tables. **Fluid volumes, airway decisions
    and transfer thresholds come from your burn protocol and the OMD** — confirm them before
    acting on any number on this page.

## Adult Rule of Nines (quick field estimate)

| Region | % of body surface |
|---|---|
| Head and neck (whole) | 9 |
| Each arm (whole) | 9 |
| Front of trunk | 18 |
| Back of trunk | 18 |
| Each leg (whole) | 18 |
| Perineum / genitalia | 1 |

Nine times: 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 (arms+head) + 18 + 18 + 18 + 18 (trunk+legs) + 1 = **100**.

- **Rule of Palms:** the patient's own palm **including fingers** ≈ **1%** — useful for small
  or scattered patches.
- **Deep partial and full-thickness burns** are counted for surface area; superficial
  (first-degree, erythema only, e.g. sunburn) is **not** counted in TBSA.

## Why children are not little adults (Lund-Browder principle)

An infant's head is proportionally large and the legs small, so adult nines
**over-estimate the head and under-estimate the legs** in a child. Approximate head and
leg shares by age:

| Age | Head (whole) | Each leg (whole) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | 18 | 13.5 |
| 5 years | 15 | 16 |
| 10 years | 13 | 17 |
| Adult | 9 | 18 |

Everything else (arms, anterior trunk, posterior trunk, perineum) is close enough to the
adult values that the Rule of Nines still works in practice.

## Burn depth language that transfers cleanly

| Term | What it describes |
|---|---|
| Superficial | Epidermis only; red, blanches, painful (sunburn) |
| Partial thickness | Dermis involved; blisters, wet, **very** painful, blanches |
| Deep partial | Mottled, weeping, may blanch slowly, pain variable |
| Full thickness | Leathery/waxy, dry, does not blanch, painful to pressure not pinprick |

**Pain is not a reliable depth sign** — a burned area that hurts less may be burned deeper.

## What to communicate on handoff

Estimated **%TBSA**, the method you used (nines vs palm), **depth by region**, airway
concerns (face burns, singed nasal hair, soot, hoarseness, enclosed-space smoke),
**time since burn**, and total fluid already given with times.
