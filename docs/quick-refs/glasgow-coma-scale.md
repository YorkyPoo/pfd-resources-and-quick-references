# Glasgow Coma Scale

**Reference scale, not a standing order.** Scoring tells you *how much* brain function is
depressed; disposition and airway decisions come from your protocol and your medical director.

!!! warning "Verify before you act on it"
    Use this card to score and to communicate. Clinical thresholds — including any
    "intubate at GCS 8" shortcut — are governed by **Poquoson Fire & Rescue protocols and
    the OMD**, not by this page.

## The three components

Score the **best** response in each category. Total = **3 to 15** (a 3 means no response at
all — never 0).

| | Eye opening (E) | Verbal response (V) | Motor response (M) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6** | — | — | Obeys commands |
| **5** | Spontaneous | Oriented | Localizes pain |
| **4** | To speech/sound | Confused | Withdraws (normal flexion) |
| **3** | To pressure/pain | Inappropriate words | Abnormal flexion (decorticate) |
| **2** | None | Incomprehensible sounds | Extension (decerebrate) |
| **1** | — | None | None |

Write it as component scores plus the total, e.g. **E3 V4 M5 = 12**.

## Pediatric verbal score (pre-verbal child)

| Score | Response |
|---|---|
| 5 | Coos, babbles appropriately |
| 4 | Irritable cry, inconsistent |
| 3 | Cries to pain |
| 2 | Moans to pain |
| 1 | None |

## Practical notes that change the number

- **Intubated or airway device in place:** verbal cannot be tested — record **V1T** (or
  "GCS with tube") and keep using E and M. Do not call a 12 with a tube a "deterioration".
- **Swollen eyes / facial trauma:** record the eye score as untestable (e.g. **E1c**) rather
  than scoring the closed eye as "none".
- **Asymmetric motor response:** record the **best** response, and mention the asymmetry — a
  one-sided deficit is a different story from a global one.
- **Sedation, paralytics, hypothermia, hypoglycaemia, post-ictal state** all depress the
  score. Note what you found and what was given; a trend across repeats matters more than one
  number.
- **Infants:** eye opening and motor response track better than verbal; use
  **AVPU** or the triage protocol's pediatric scale if it asks for one, and say which you used.
- Report a **trend with times** to the receiving team — "GCS 15 → 12 over 20 minutes" is
  actionable; "GCS 12" alone is not.
