# Employee Performance Review

_**Section No.: 11**_

## Employee Performance Review

Annually all supervisors/Department Heads shall evaluate and discuss the overall job
performance with each employee. Attendance, punctuality, quantity of work, quality of
work, knowledge of the work being performed, initiative and relationships with fellow
employees and the public will be evaluated.

The employee may submit a rebuttal statement as part of the evaluation and such
statement shall remain in the employee’s personnel file as long as the evaluation is
retained on file.
