# Annual Leave (VRS Plan 1, Plan 2 and

_**Section No.: 15**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to provide employees with paid
annual leave for vacations and other personal purposes.

## Scope

This policy applies to all City of Poquoson permanent full-time employees.
## Definitions

Annual Leave

Annual leave is defined as an employee absence during regularly scheduled work hours
that has been approved, by the proper supervisory authority, for vacation or other
personal purposes.

VRS Plan 1 Employees - Employees hired prior to July 1, 2010 and were vested in VRS
as of January 1, 2013.

VRS Plan 2 Employees - Employees hired on or after July !, 2010 and before January 1,
2014 or an employee who was hired prior to July 1 2010 who left employment, withdrew
all VRS contributions and then was rehired or an employee who was not vested prior to
January 1, 2013.

VRS Hybrid Employees — Employees hired, except hazardous duty employees, on or
after January 1, 2014 with no prior VRS creditable service or account balance ina VRS
administered optional retirement plan or Plan | and Plan 2 employees who opt into the
hybrid plan.

## Procedures

A, Annual Leave Requests and Approval

1.

Employees wishing to use annual leave must request approval from their
supervisor, or supervisor’s designee.

2. Requests for annual leave must be approved in advance, if possible, except in the

case of an emergency and according to individual departmental policies. An
employee seeking to use his or her annual leave shall submit a Leave Request
Form to their Department Head at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance of the
first day of requested leave. Requests for annual leave of more than two (2)
consecutive working days must be submitted to the Department Head at least
one (1) week in advance of the first day of requested leave, if possible. All
requests for use of annual leave are subject to approval by the Department Head,
who in his/her sole discretion, may approve use of annual leave with shorter
notice than specified in this section, if circumstances warrant.

When approving or disapproving annual leave requests, supervisors shall consider
the work requirements of the department.

Any employee absence without appropriate supervisory approval shall be
considered unauthorized.

B. Annual Leave Accrual and Carryover

1.

Employees shall not use annuat leave until it is accrued. Newly hired employees
who work one-half (1/2) of the month shall accrue the full allotted amount of
leave.

Annual leave accrues at the end of cach month.

Annual leave does not accrue for any month unless the employee is compensated
for at least one-half (1/2) of the working days in that month.

C Using (Debiting) Annual Leave

1. Annual leave shall be debited in no less than one-half (1/2) hour units.

2. Each department shall forward to the Personnel Office approved annual
leave records indicating annual leave debits and/or credits for employees. The

records shall include approved annual leave request forms for all annual leave
taken.

3. For VRS Hybrid Plan employees only: Annual leave can be used to

supplement short-term or long-term disability income up to 100% of the
employee’s wages at time of use.

D. Treatment of Accrued Annual Leave Upon Change in Status

1. Lateral Transfer, Promotion or Demotion

Annual leave balances held by employees at the time of lateral transfer,
promotion or demotion from one position or department to another shall be
retained by the employees.

2. Voluntary Separation

Upon voluntary separation, employees shall be paid a lump sum for the unused
portion of their accrued annual leave balances not to exceed the maximum
authorized compensable balances specified in Section B.4 of this policy.

3. Resignation Without Sufficient Notice

a. Employees who resign without satisfactory notice may lose up to ten days
of accumulated compensable annual leave.

b. If accumulated annual leave is withheld as a result of resignation without
satisfactory notice, employees will be paid for any remaining annual leave
balances up to the maximum compensable balance levels.

4. Unsatisfactory Service Separation
Employees who are separated as a result of unsatisfactory service may be paid

for unused accrued annual leave up to the maximum compensable balance if
the City Manager determines that the payment is appropriate.
