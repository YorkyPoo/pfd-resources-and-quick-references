# Employee Service Awards Program

_**Section No.: 25**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to recognize and commend faithful
service performed by employees; to emphasize that each individual plays a key role in the
City’s progress; to recognize that an employee’s contribution grows with each additional
year of service and to encourage career employment with the City of Poquoson.

## Scope

This policy applies to all City of Poquoson permanent full-time and permanent part-time
employees.

## Procedures

Continuous Service Awards

|. The Employee Service Awards Program shall be held annually, normally in
December of each year or as soon thereafter as possible.

2. The award year shall coincide with the calendar year (January through
December).

3. The City shall give awards for length of service beginning with completion of the
5 year and for each five year increment of service thereafter.

4. Gifts for each category of service will be determined at the time the award is to be
given.
