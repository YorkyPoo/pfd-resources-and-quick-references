# Salary Increases

_**Section No.: 12**_

## Administrative Salary Increase

An administrative salary increase shall be defined as a salary increase within the pay of a
class that is awarded to an employee displaying exceptionally outstanding meritorious
service. Employees may be recommended for an administrative increase upon
submission of a letter of justification by the respective Department Head to the City
Manager. An administrative increase shall only be awarded to regular or regular part-
time employees.

## Stipend

A stipend is defined as a temporary salary increase within a pay range for employees that
assume special job responsibilities.

## Supplemental Pay

Supplemental pay is a non-permanent augmentation of compensation based upon a
specific duty(s), certification or other circumstance wherein the performance of said duty
or the achievement of the advanced certification is not a permanent requirement of the
position but in any case directly benefits the organization and/or the public.
