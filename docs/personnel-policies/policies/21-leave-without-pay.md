# Leave Without Pay ~

_**Section No.: 21**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to provide employees with unpaid
time off from work for specified circumstances.

## Scope

This policy applies to ali permanent full-time employees.
## Definition

Leave Without Pay

Leave Without Pay is defined as an approved or unapproved absence from work without
pay.

## Procedures
Unapproved Absences

An unapproved employee absence may be charged to leave without pay, as determined
by the employee’s supervisor, or supervisor’s designee.

Leave Without Pay Requests

An employee wishing to use leave without pay must request approval from his/her
Department Head/Constitutional Officer. Requests for leave without pay should be made
as far in advance as possible.

1. Leave Without Pay Requests — Periods of Ten (10) Calendar Days or Less

An employee’s Department Head/Constitutional Officer may approve leave
without pay requests for periods of ten (10) calendar days or less.

2.

3.

Leave Without Pay Requests — Periods in Excess of Ten (10) Calendar Days

a. Leave Without Pay requests for periods in excess of ten (10) calendar days
must be submitted to the employee’s Department Head/Constitutional Officer.

b. If approved, the request shall be forwarded to the City Manager for review
and final approval/disapproval.

Leave Without Pay may be granted for a maximum period of twelve (12) months
from the start of the leave.

Eligibility to Use Leave Without Pay

1.

Except for events designated as Family/Medical Leave (FMLA)

a. An employee must exhaust all accrued annual leave balances before using
leave without pay.

b. In cases of illness, an employee must exhaust all accrued annual leave,
compensatory leave and all but one week of sick leave before using leave
without pay. (One week of sick leave shall be calculated based upon the
employee’s normally scheduled workweek.)

Verification of the Need for Leave Without Pay

1.

An employee wishing to use leave without pay must comply with management
requests for verification of need.

Leave Without Pay Stipulations

I.

Annual and sick leave do not accrue during leave without pay status unless the
employee is compensated for at least one-half (1/2) of the working days in the
months that leave without pay is taken.

Leave without pay shall not constitute a break in service if an employee returns
to work at the expiration of the approved leave period.

. A probationary employee who takes leave without pay may have his/her

probationary period extended for the length of the leave.

. Upon return from leave without pay, an employee will be placed in his/her

former position unless there has been a change in staffing levels or staff
organization which necessitates the assignment of the employee to an available
position for which he/she is qualified.

. Leave without pay may affect an employee’s performance evaluation.

6. Failure of an employee to report for duty at the expiration of the leave without
pay period may be cause for dismissal. The effective date of termination will be
the last day worked.

7. All costs associated with medical insurance will be the responsibility of the
employee.
