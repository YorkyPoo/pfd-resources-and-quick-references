# Equal Employment Opportunity

_**Section No.: 5**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to adhere to a policy of equal
employment opportunity for all employees and applicants for employment. Personnel
management and employment practices will be conducted without regard to race, color,
religion, national origin, political affiliation, disability, age or sex. Discrimination is
prohibited in the areas of recruitment, hiring, training, promotion, retention, discipline
and/or any other aspect of personnel administration.

## Scope

This policy applies to all City of Poquoson employees and applicants for employment.
## Responsibility

Department Heads and others with supervisory authority shall take affirmative measures
to ensure that all personnel actions are executed without regard to race, color, religion,

national origin, political affiliation, disability, age or sex.

The City Manager is responsible for the administration of the Equal Employment
Opportunity policy.

## Procedures
Notices stating that the City of Poquoson is an Equal Employment Opportunity Employer
shall be prominently displayed in City Offices/Departments and placed on all

applications for employment.

Allegations of violations of this policy should be filed with the City Manager.
