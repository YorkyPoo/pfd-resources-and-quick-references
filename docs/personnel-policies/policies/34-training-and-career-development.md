# Training and Career Development

_**Section No.: 34**_

## Purpose

The City of Poquoson recognizes that well-trained and educated employees enhance City
services. It is the City’s objective to provide employee training and career development
in support of City employees and operations.

## Scope

This policy applies to permanent employees. In addition, full-time temporary employees
may be provided training, if it is deemed beneficial to agency operations.

## Definitions

City-wide Training

City-wide training is defined as training funded from a dedicated City reserve. City-wide
training consists of general training applicable to the majority of the workforce such as
supervisory training, interpersonal skills development, computer application software
classes, etc.

Department-specific Training

Department-specific training is defined as training funded in an individual departmental
budget line for needs relating to the specific work requirements of the department.

## Procedures
Providing Training Opportunities
1. Training and career development opportunities shall be made available in an

equitable manner without regard to race, color, religion, national origin, political
affiliation, disability, age or sex.

2. Employee training and career development activities must be related to

organizational goals and objectives and enhance employee job performance.

Funding

1.

As funding and resources are available, the City will offer training and career
development programs which address organizational needs, skill development and
acquisition of knowledge.

Expenditures for any individual employee to attend any single training or career
development opportunity shall not exceed $1,000. The limit is inclusive of
travel, registration and other costs associated with the training or career
development opportunity.

In those instances which may arise where funds for certain training or career
development exceed the $1,000 limit referenced in paragraph 2 above, the
Department Head/Constitutional Officer may make an appeal for an exception to
the policy. Such an appeal must demonstrate good cause and must be made to the
City Manager.

Performance Evaluation Process

Training and career development should be an integral component of the performance
evaluation and goal setting process between an employee and his/her supervisor.

Records Maintenance

A copy of all training and career development courses completed by employees requiring
official City approval for expenditure of City funds shall be maintained in employee
personnel records.
