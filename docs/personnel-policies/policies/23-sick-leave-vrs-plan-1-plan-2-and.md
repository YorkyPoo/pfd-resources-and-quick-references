# Sick Leave (VRS Plan 1, Plan 2 and

_**Section No.: 23**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to provide employees with paid
leave from work for legitimate health related reasons.

## Scope
This policy applies to all City of Poquoson permanent full-time employees.
## Definitions

VRS Plan 1 Employees — Employees hired prior to July |, 2010 and were vested in VRS
as of January 1, 2013.

VRS Plan 2 Employees — Employees hired on or after July 1, 2010 and before January 1,
2014. or an employee who was hired prior to July 1, 2010 who left employment,
withdrew all VRS contributions and then was rehired, or an employee who was not
vested prior to January 1, 2013.

VRS Hybrid Employees — Employees hired, except hazardous duty employees, on or
after January 1, 2014 with no prior VRS creditable service or account balance in a VRS
administered optional retirement plan or Plan | and Plan 2 employees who opt into the
hybrid plan.

Sick Leave

Sick leave is defined as an approved absence during regularly scheduled work hours for
the following reasons:

1. an illness or a medical necessity, during an employee’s temporary disability from
performing his/her job duties, including disability relating to pregnancy or
childbirth;

if an employee has, or has been exposed to, a contagious disease such that his/her
presence on the job might jeopardize the health of others; and/or

if an employee’s medical or dental appointment(s) cannot be scheduled during
non-work hours.

## Procedures

A, Sick Leave Requests and Approval

1.

Employees wishing to use sick leave must request approval from their supervisor,
or supervisor’s designee.

Employees who have prior knowledge of an impending need to use sick leave
(i.e., for operations; special medical, dental, or optical treatments; etc.) shall
make the request for sick leave as far in advance as possible.

Upon exhaustion of sick leave balances, employees may request approval to use
accrued annual and/or compensatory leave.

Employee absences without appropriate supervisory approval shall be considered
unauthorized.

B. Verification of the Need for Sick Leave

lL.

Employees wishing to use sick leave must comply with management requests for
verification of need.

2. Employees may be required to submit verification at any time.

Failure to comply with management requests for verification may result in
disapproval of sick leave.

Forms of verification include, but are not limited to, the following:

a.

certification from the treating physician that the employee is temporarily
disabled and is unable to work. The physician’s certification should include
the extent of the employee’s disability and the estimated period of disability;

certification from the treating physician that the employee’s immediate family
member is temporarily disabled. The physician’s certification should include
the extent of the immediate family member’s disability and the estimated
period of disability;

certification from the treating physician that the employee currently has or has
been exposed to a contagious disease such that his/her presence on the job
might jeopardize the health of others; or

evidence that the employee or employee’s immediate family member has a
medical appointment that could not have been scheduled during non-work
hours.

Employees may be required to take a medical examination at the expense of the
City.

Before returning to work from sick leave, employees may be required to submit a
physician’s certification indicating fitness to return.

C. Family Sick Leave

The use of family sick leave shall be debited against employee sick leave balances.

D. Maternity Leave

Employees may utilize accrued sick leave to take leave after the birth of a child
immediately following the birth. Such leave shall run concurrently
with Family/Medical leave (FMLA).

E. Sick Leave Accrual

l.

Employees participating in VRS as Plan 1 and Plan 2 employees shall accrue sick
leave at the rate of one work day per month (12 days per year). For Fire
Personnel this equates to 12 days per year; for all other employees, 96 hours per
year.

5.

Employees participating in VRS as Hybrid Plan employees shall accrue sick leave
at a rate of 10 days per year which equates to 6.75 hours per month for all
employees.

Sick leave accrues at the end of each month.
Sick leave does not accrue for any month unless employee is compensated for at
least one-half (1/2) of the working days in that month. New employees who work

one-half (I/2) of the month, will accrue the full amount of leave for that month.

Employees may accrue unlimited amounts of sick leave.

G. Treatment of Sick Leave Upon Change in Status

1,

Upon lateral transfer, promotion, or demotion from one position or department to
another, employees shall retain prior accrued sick leave balances.

Upon voluntary separation, a Plan 1, Plan 2 or Hybrid employee with five (5) or
more years of service shall be paid fifty dollars ($50) per one (1) work day of
accumulated sick leave or may apply the credit to the payment of health
insurance premiums associated with COBRA. Such sick leave credits shall be
made on an after-tax (net pay) basis. Total amount paid to a Plan | or Plan 2
employee shall not exceed $6,000. Total amount paid to a VRS Hybrid Plan
employee shall not exceed $3,000.
