# Physical Examinations For High

_**Section No.: 38**_

Section No.: 38 Supersedes Policy:

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to ensure that post-offer candidates
for employment and current employees occupying high-risk positions are physically fit to
perform the functions of the positions.

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and permanent part-time employees who are post-offer
candidates for employment and current employees who occupy high-risk positions.

## Definitions
High-Risk Positions
A high-risk position is defined as:
e Any position in which the employee’s sudden incapacitation shall likely
immediately result in additional injury to the employee, other employees and/or

citizens.

e Any position in which work assignments normally involve more than fifty (50)
percent manual labor.

e Any position that requires physical prowess.

## Procedures

Designation of High-Risk Positions

lL.

Department Heads/Constitutional Officers shall determine if there are high-risk
positions in their departments and forward a list of such positions to the City
Manager.

The City Manager shall approve such high-risk designations based upon a review
of duties and responsibilities, working conditions and physical demand factors as
described in the position description.

Post Job offer Physical Examinations

L.

Physical examinations shall occur after a job offer is made and before the
employment candidate begins work.

It is the responsibility of the hiring department to schedule physical examinations.
Cost of Examinations/Evaluations

(a) The City shall bear the cost of any and all examinations/evaluations required
under this policy.

(b) The City shall bear the cost of any additional examinations/evaluations
required as follow-up to post-offer employment physicals.

(c) The employment candidate shall bear the cost of any and all follow-up
examinations/evaluations beyond those required by the City to determine
fitness for a high-risk position.

A licensed physician selected by the City shall perform all physical
examinations/evaluations paid for by the City.

. All examinations/evaluations not paid for by the City that are used to satisfy the

requirements of this policy shall meet the standards set forth by the City and shall
be provided to the City’s selected physician.

. Upon completion of the examination, the City’s selected physician shall return

the completed Physician’s Certification form to the appropriate Department
Head/Constitutional Officer who shall forward the form to the Personnel Officer
for inclusion in the confidential personnel files.

7. The determination of whether an employment candidate shall be authorized to
begin work shall be based upon the recommendation of the examining physician.

Employee Physical Examinations

1. Employees in high-risk positions may be required to undergo triennial or more
frequent physical examinations to determine fitness to continue in their positions.

2. The procedures outlined above shall apply to employee physical examinations.
3. The determination of whether an employee shall be authorized to continue in

his/her position or shall be temporarily or permanently reassigned, if appropriate,
shall be based upon the recommendation of the examining physician.
