# Smoking In The Workplace

_**Section No.: 37**_

## Purpose

It is the objective of the City of Poquoson, Virginia to protect the health, comfort, and
environment of City employees and visitors by creating areas in City buildings and at
public meetings that are smoke free.

“Smoking” means inhaling, exhaling, burning or carrying any lighted smoking equipment
for tobacco to include e-cigarettes.

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees and volunteers.
## Procedures

Prohibited Areas

For reasons of safety, public relations or other concerns, smoking is prohibited in all
public lobbies, offices and other areas within City buildings as well as in all City
vehicles.

Designated Smoking Areas

A designated smoking area outside public buildings and work areas will be identified by
the City. Said areas will be away from entrances that are visible to the public.

Visitors

Visitors to any office area are expected to observe non-smoking designations.

The City Manager shall cause conspicuous signs to be posted in designated areas where
smoking is permitted. Areas where smoking is not permitted may have signs posted, as
appropriate.

Public Safety Employees

Public Safety Employees (Police and Fire) shall be required to sign a no smoking (on or
off duty) pledge. The purpose of the pledge is to minimize the employee’s risk to certain
diseases while recognizing the employee’s risk and rights as stated in Section 65.2-402 of
the Code of Virginia, as amended, titled “Presumption as to death or disability from
respiratory disease, hypertension or heart disease, cancer...”
