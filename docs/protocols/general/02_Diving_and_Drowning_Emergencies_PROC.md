# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## General - Diving and Drowning Emergencies

### Overview
Guidelines for managing acute submersion, non-fatal drowning, and decompression injuries, emphasizing airway management, rewarming, and specialized transport.

### Assessment & Criteria
=== "History of Present Illness"
    - Submersion
    - Compressed air diving

=== "Signs & Symptoms"
    - Altered mental status (AMS)
    - Seizures
    - Vision changes
    - Respiratory distress
    - Hemoptysis
    - Chest pain
    - Headache
    - Focal neurologic signs
    - Ataxia
    - Pain in joints / extremities

=== "Differential Considerations"
    - Hypothermia
    - Pulmonary barotrauma
    - Arterial gas embolism
    - Decompression sickness

### Interventions

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol. Remove the patient from the water. |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Consider spinal motion restriction. Refer to Spinal Motion Restriction procedure. |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Assess airway and ventilation. Refer to Airway Management and Ventilatory Support protocol. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Remove wet clothing and dry the skin. Keep the patient warm. |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | If hypothermia and hemodynamically unstable, focus effort on rewarming as well as resuscitation simultaneously. |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Diving emergencies: Assess for pulmonary barotrauma, decompression sickness, and/or arterial gas embolism. Refer to the appropriate protocol(s) as needed. |
| 7 | <span class="lvl lvl-mc">MC</span> | Contact Medical Control for hyperbaric transport destination guidance if decompression injury is suspected. |

### Pearls
1. ⚠ Do not initiate transport to a hyperbaric center without confirming chamber availability first.
2. ⚠ In hypothermia with hemodynamic instability, focus on both rewarming and resuscitation simultaneously.
