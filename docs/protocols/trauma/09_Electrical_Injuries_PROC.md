# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## General - Electrical Injuries

### Overview
Guidelines for managing electrical contact and lightning injuries, emphasizing scene safety, reverse triage, and specific fluid resuscitation.

### Assessment & Criteria
=== "History of Present Illness"
    - Electrical contact
    - Lightning strike

=== "Signs & Symptoms"
    - Burns
    - Cardiac arrest
    - Arrhythmias

=== "Differential Considerations"
    - Scene safety
    - Reverse triage

### Interventions
<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Ensure scene safety / patient not energized. Expose area and remove constricting items. |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Multiple patients: Treat cardiac arrest FIRST (reverse triage) |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Assess airway (manage by least invasive means necessary) |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-emt">EMT</span> | Estimate TBSA/burn depth (2nd/3rd degree only) |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-emt">EMT</span> | Apply clean dry dressings. Prevent loss of body heat and keep patient warm. |
| 7 | <span class="lvl lvl-a">A</span><sup class="lvl-own" title="OWNER_REQUIRED: no PEMS 2024 row authorises this action's provider level">&#9873;</sup> | Obtain 12-Lead ECG / Monitor rhythm continuously |

| 9 | <span class="lvl lvl-a">A</span> | SBP < 90 & TBSA > 20%:<br>• Adult: **0.9% Sodium Chloride 500 mL/hr IV/IO**<br>• Ped (6-12y): **0.9% Sodium Chloride 250 mL/hr IV/IO**<br>• Ped (≤5y): **0.9% Sodium Chloride 125 mL/hr IV/IO** |

### Pearls
1. ⚠ Ensure scene safety and patient is not energized - DO NOT become a second victim.
2. ⚠ Lightning strikes with multiple victims require REVERSE triage (treat cardiac arrest first).
