# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## Administrative - Helicopter EMS (HEMS)

### Overview
Guidelines and indications for utilizing Helicopter EMS for patient transport and specialty care.

### Assessment & Criteria
=== "History of Present Illness"
    - STEMI
    - Stroke
    - Trauma
    - Burns
    - Critical Pediatrics
    - Ventricular Assist Device (LVAD/BiVAD/VAD)

=== "Signs & Symptoms"
    - Wilderness rescue
    - Ambulance egress or access impeded at the scene by road conditions, weather, or traffic

=== "Differential Considerations"
    - The air ambulance service can provide needed medical capability at the scene

### Interventions
<div class="lv-wrap policy" markdown="1">

| # | Basis | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Request HEMS:** Request air ambulance standby/response according to department procedure (no medical control authorization needed). |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Establish Landing Zone:** Establish a safe and logistically feasible landing zone. |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Provide Patient Info:** Establish number of patients, primary injuries/medical condition, and approximate patient weight(s). Forward this information to the responding air medical service. |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Prepare Patient:** Trauma patients should be fully immobilized. **IV/IO** access should be established as time allows (within provider scope). |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Designate Reporter:** On scene, designate one individual who will be responsible for providing the patient report(s) to the flight crew upon arrival. |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-policy">POLICY</span> | **Provide Documentation:** Ensure PCR is provided to receiving facility as per 12 VAC 5-31. |

</div>

<p class="lv-note"><strong>POLICY</strong> — rows 1-6: no provider level applies. PEMS publishes these rules as administrative policy, not as a clinical protocol: each is cited to the PEMS 2024 administrative policy that prints it — <em>Helicopter EMS (HEMS)</em> (PEMS p.23) — and no provider level is published, because PEMS prints these rules as policy rather than as level-badged protocol rows.</p>

### Pearls
* ⚠ VAD patients require coordination with VAD Coordinator or Medical Control.
* HEMS requested via department procedure without online medical control authorization.
