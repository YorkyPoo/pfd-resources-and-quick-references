# Poquoson Fire and Rescue Patient Care Protocols
## General - V-Fib / Pulseless V-Tach

Guidelines for the management of pulseless ventricular tachycardia and ventricular fibrillation, emphasizing high-quality CPR and rapid defibrillation.

### Assessment & Criteria
=== "History of Present Illness"
    - Unresponsive
    - Apneic
    - Pulseless

=== "Signs & Symptoms"
    - VF/VT on monitor or AED advise shock

=== "Differential Considerations"
    - Valid DDNR/Obvious death exclusions
    - Reversible causes

### Interventions

<div class="lv-wrap" markdown="1">

| # | Level | Action / Intervention |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Begin high-quality CPR / determine DNR status |
| 2 | <span class="lvl lvl-emt">EMT</span> | Consider spinal motion restriction |
| 3 | <span class="lvl lvl-a">A</span> | Establish IV/IO (Humeral IO preferred) |
| 4 | <span class="lvl lvl-a">A</span> | Fluid Resuscitation: **0.9% Sodium Chloride up to 1 L** (Adult) / **20 mL/kg** (Ped) |
| 5 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Defibrillation — AED: apply the AED and defibrillate when the AED advises a shock (the device selects the energy). |
| 5a | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Defibrillation — Manual: **200 J Biphasic / 360 J Monophasic** (Adult) / **2 J/kg initial, 4 J/kg subsequent** (Ped) |
| 6 | <span class="lvl lvl-a">A</span> | **[Epinephrine](../../pharmacology/Epinephrine.md) 1 mg (0.1mg/mL) IV/IO** (Adult) / **0.01 mg/kg IV/IO** (Ped) q3-5 mins |
| 7 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | First Antiarrhythmic: **[Amiodarone](../../pharmacology/Amiodarone.md) 300 mg IV/IO** (Adult) / **5 mg/kg** (Ped max 300mg) OR **[Lidocaine](../../pharmacology/Lidocaine.md) 1 mg/kg IV/IO** (Adult max 100mg, Ped max 50mg) |
| 8 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Second Antiarrhythmic: **[Amiodarone](../../pharmacology/Amiodarone.md) 150 mg IV/IO** (Adult) / **5 mg/kg** (Ped max 150mg, Medical Control authorisation required) OR **[Lidocaine](../../pharmacology/Lidocaine.md) 0.5 mg/kg IV/IO** (Adult max 100mg) / **1 mg/kg** (Ped max 50mg) |
| 9 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Persistent VF/VT: **[Magnesium Sulfate](../../pharmacology/Magnesium%20Sulfate.md) 2 g IV/IO** (Adult) / **40 mg/kg** (Ped max 2g, Medical Control authorisation required) |
| 10 | <span class="lvl lvl-i">I</span> | Dialysis/Crush/Hyperkalemia: **[Calcium Chloride](../../pharmacology/Calcium%20Chloride.md) 1 g IV/IO** (Adult) / **20 mg/kg** (Ped max 1g) THEN flush THEN **[Sodium Bicarbonate](../../pharmacology/Sodium%20Bicarbonate.md) 50 mEq IV/IO** (Adult) / **1 mEq/kg** (Ped max 50 mEq) |
| 11 | <span class="lvl lvl-emr">EMR</span> | Proceed to Post ROSC Care if pulses return |
| 12 | <span class="lvl lvl-mc">MC</span> | Contact Medical Control for guidance / termination of resuscitation |

</div>

### Pearls
• High-quality, uninterrupted CPR is the most important intervention in cardiac arrest.
• Ensure optimal defibrillator pad placement.
• ⚠ Flush lines well between **[Calcium Chloride](../../pharmacology/Calcium%20Chloride.md)** and **[Sodium Bicarbonate](../../pharmacology/Sodium%20Bicarbonate.md)** to prevent precipitation.
• Reversible causes (H's and T's).
