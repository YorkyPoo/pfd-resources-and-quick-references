# Ondansetron

**Trade Name:** Zofran, Zofran ODT

## Classification & Action

- **Drug Class:** Antiemetic
- **Mechanism of Action:** Relieves nausea and vomiting.

## Indications

- Nausea and vomiting
- Pain management (prophylactic)

## Contraindications

- Allergy / hypersensitivity
- Prolonged QTc (>500 ms)

## Precautions

- Less effective if used after vomiting has begun.
- Assess risk in patients with other QT-prolonging medications or pre-existing prolonged QTc.

## Dosage

### Adults

- **EMT - ODT:** 4 mg ODT. May repeat in 10-15 minutes as needed.
- **ALS - IV/IO/IM/PO:** 4 mg IV/IO slow push, IM, or PO. For IV/IO administration, give by slow push for at least 30 seconds; preferably over 2 minutes.
- **Maximum total dose:** 8 mg.

### Pediatrics

- **Patients >1 year:** 0.15 mg/kg IV/IO slow push, IM, or PO; maximum 4 mg per dose.
- May repeat in 10-15 minutes as needed.
- **Maximum total dose:** 0.3 mg/kg.

## Pharmacokinetics

| Route | Onset / Peak Effect | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IV/IM | Rapid; peak effect in 15-30 minutes | IV: 2-4 hours |
| ODT | Rapid; peak effect in 1-2 hours | ODT: 12-24 hours |

## Side Effects

- QT prolongation
- Headache
- Drowsiness

## Interactions

1. Additive effects with medications that prolong the QT interval.
2. Additive CNS depressant effects.

## Pearls

- **Warning:** Contraindicated if QTc is prolonged (>500 ms).
- Less effective if used after vomiting has begun.
- Use caution with other QT-prolonging medications, including amiodarone and haloperidol.
