# F.O.R.C.E.S. — First-Alarm Company Mnemonic (EO 6.00)

!!! abstract "What this is"
    A nested mnemonic where the top-level letters **F.O.R.C.E.S.** match the **arrival order** of the first-alarm companies, and each letter expands into that apparatus's chronological tactical checklist. Derived from [EO 6.00 First To Arrive Duties](EO-600-First-to-Arrive-Duties.md) and [Initial Alarm Company Assignments](../../fire-ems-references/initial-alarm-company-assignments.md).
    
    **It is a drill and teaching tool, not a fireground checklist.** On the street, run CAN, RECEO-VS, SLICE-RS, and COAL WAS WEALTH — single-level cues survive adrenaline; nested ones only work after repetition has made the sub-lists automatic.

## The Top Level

| Letter | Sub-mnemonic     | Unit & role                                  |
| :----- | :--------------- | :------------------------------------------- |
| **F**  | **F.A.S.T.E.R.** | 1st engine (Tower 1) — command & attack      |
| **O**  | **O.P.E.N.S.**   | 2nd engine (Squad 2) — water & backup line   |
| **R**  | **R.A.I.L.**     | 3rd engine (Engine 3) — RIT                  |
| **C**  | **C.L.E.A.R.**   | Aerial interior (Ladder 1) — search & rescue |
| **E**  | **E.X.T.R.A.**   | Aerial exterior (Ladder 1) — ladders & vent  |
| **S**  | **S.T.A.T.**     | Squad (Squad 3) — support priorities         |

## F.A.S.T.E.R. — First Engine

- **F** — **First transmission:** IRR — unit ID, on-scene confirmation, building description, conditions, strategy, actions, command established/named, tactical channel assignment, working-fire declaration (§VIII).
- **A** — **Assume command** and conduct continuous size-up (§VI, §VII — COAL WAS WEALTH).
- **S** — **Strategy** and operational mode declared: Offensive / Transitional / Defensive (§IX).
- **T** — **Three-sixty** completed when conditions and staffing permit (§VII).
- **E** — **Extend** the initial attack line; **execute** SLICE-RS and rescue where a survivability profile exists.
- **R** — **Receive** water supply where practical / tank water in service — explicit handoff to the second engine (§X).

## O.P.E.N.S. — Second Engine

- **O** — **Outline** the water supply plan on the radio (Who / Where / What).
- **P** — **Primary** water supply established (hydrant, dual/forward lay).
- **E** — **Extend** the second handline — back up the attack line, protect stairs/egress.
- **N** — **Note** and assess exposures.
- **S** — **Secondary** supply identified (tank water, relay, draft).

## R.A.I.L. — Third Engine (RIT)

- **R** — **RIT established** and announced (§XIV — mandatory whenever crews operate in hazardous environments).
- **A** — **Add ladders** — ground ladders staged for rescue and RIT entry.
- **I** — **Isolate utilities** (gas/electric) — ownership sits here, nowhere else.
- **L** — **Lookout** assigned for continuous exterior assessment.

## C.L.E.A.R. — First Aerial, Interior Crew

- **C** — **Confirm** conditions and flow path before entry (door state, coordinated vent plan).
- **L** — **Life safety first** — immediate rescues in survivable spaces (SIGNAL profiling).
- **E** — **Execute** primary search ahead of / with the hose line.
- **A** — **Augment** with door-to-door isolation and VEIS.
- **R** — **Remove** victims / report all clear.

## E.X.T.R.A. — First Aerial, Exterior Driver

- **E** — **Evaluate** exposure sides and report (utilities, collapse zone, overheads).
- **X** — e**X**tend ground ladders — secondary egress at Division 2 windows.
- **T** — **Track** utilities — monitor and report only; control is assigned to R.A.I.L./third engine.
- **R** — **Regulate** airflow — natural/horizontal ventilation coordinated *after* water on fire.
- **A** — **Aerial set up** — turntable on the address side; engines leave it room (§XV).

## S.T.A.T. — Squad

- **S** — **Support** search and forcible entry — augment the aerial crew.
- **T** — **Transport** tools, ladders, and equipment to where they're needed.
- **A** — **Assist** accountability — PAR tracking, personnel accounting (§XIII).
- **T** — **Transition** to salvage, overhaul, and water removal (§XI).

## Deliberate Exclusions & Caveats

1. **Transfer of command and PAR** are *command* functions (§VI, §XIII), not company tasks — they live in the Command note, not in a company mnemonic.
2. **Sequence is apparatus-dependent.** FORCES describes the Poquoson first-alarm assignment. If the aerial arrives second or a tankered engine lands in the box, remap **by function, not by arrival letter**.
3. **"X" is a mnemonic hack** (eXtend). Acceptable for recall, but don't teach the letter as the lesson.
4. Utility control appears **once** (R.A.I.L. — I). The aerial driver *tracks*, never *terminates* — double-booked tasks are how utilities get missed or duplicated on the street.
