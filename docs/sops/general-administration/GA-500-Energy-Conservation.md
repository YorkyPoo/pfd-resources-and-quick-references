# GA 5.00 Energy Conservation

<div class="sop-meta">**SOP** GA 5.00 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Category:** General Administration &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Effective:** 11/30/2008 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Revised:** 11/30/2008</div>

## I. Purpose

To establish policy and guidelines relative to the efficient use of energy and water.

This SOP is not all-inclusive and cannot encompass all situations that may be encountered.

## II. Policy

All Fire Department personnel shall:

e Strive to conserve both energy (electricity) and water.

e Make every effort to curtail unnecessary use of energy (electricity) and water so as not to give the appearance of waste.

## III. Guidelines
Electricity Lights/electrical equipment should be

turned off or placed in standby when not being utilized.

GENERAL ADMINISTRATION

GA 5.00 Energy Conservation Heating Systems

e All thermostats within fire stations shall be set and maintained at 70 -72 degrees.

e All building doors will remain fully closed at all times.

e Apparatus bay doors will be closed immediately upon apparatus exiting or entering bay and will remain closed at all times.

Cooling Systems

e Thermostats for controlling cooling equipment in fire stations shall be set and maintained at 72-74 degrees.

e All building doors will remain fully closed at all times.

e Apparatus bay doors may remain open during warm days to keep bay cool.

Water In the event of water shortages:

e No private vehicles may be washed at fire stations. e Training activities utilizing water are suspended.
