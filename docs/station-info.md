# How to Use This Site

This page holds the orientation material and the change log, so the home page stays a
one-screen tool you can use with a glove on.

## Field basics

- **Search:** the search bar is in the header and stays visible while you scroll — it works
  with no signal once the site has loaded.
- **Getting around:** on a phone, four tabs ride along the bottom of the screen — **Home**,
  **Protocols**, **SOPs**, **Station**. The menu button just above the bar opens the full
  menu, for everything that is not one of the four.
- **Offline:** after the first load, every page is cached — basements, rural scenes, steel
  buildings.
- **Install:** add to your home screen on iOS or Android to open it like a native app.
- **Corrections:** every protocol and SOP page ends with a **Report a correction** button that
  pre-fills the page name. Submissions go to Medical Control; nothing changes without their approval.

## Where things live

- **Protocols** — clinical protocols by category, skills procedures, pharmacology, pediatric
  dosing, and the EMS SOPs.
- **SOPs** — the fire standard operating procedures.
- **Fire Resources** — frameworks, field references, strategies, and the FORCES study aid;
  reached from the SOPs page or the full menu.
- **Station** — city and personnel policies, crew links and portals, station administration.

## Reading the clinical pages

Every protocol carries the same five-pip strip, **R B A I P**. Tap your certification level at
the top of the strip — or leave it on **Show all** — and the page answers one question for every
action: do I act, do I call, or is this not mine.

- **Tap your level** and the protocol reads as *you*: each action is tagged with what you may do
  with it, the actions that are not yours dim down, and a count under the strip tells you how many
  you may perform, how many need a call, and how many are not yours.

<!-- PIP_LEGEND -->

Leave it on **Show all** and every row names the level its action starts at — *standing from B*,
*MC at I, standing from P*, *MC at every level*, *no level authorised* — so a whole protocol reads
in one pass without tapping anything.

Levels are **R** (Emergency Medical Responder) · **B** (Emergency Medical Technician) · **A**
(AEMT) · **I** (Intermediate) · **P** (Paramedic).

The authority rules — which tiers inherit, when Medical Control applies, what an empty pip really
excludes — are defined in one place: **[How to Read the Protocols](protocols/front-matter/01 - How to Read the Protocols.md)**.
Clinical numbers on this site are references, not prescriptions: **your local protocols and
Medical Control govern.**
