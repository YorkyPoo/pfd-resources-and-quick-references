# How to Use This Site

This page holds the orientation material and the change log, so the home page stays a
one-screen tool you can use with a glove on.

## Field basics

- **Search:** the search bar is in the header and stays visible while you scroll — it works
  with no signal once the site has loaded.
- **Menu:** on phones the menu button sits **bottom-right** for thumb reach.
- **Offline:** after the first load, every page is cached — basements, rural scenes, steel
  buildings.
- **Install:** add to your home screen on iOS or Android to open it like a native app.
- **Corrections:** every protocol and SOP page ends with a **Report a correction** button that
  pre-fills the page name. Submissions go to the OMD; nothing changes without their approval.

## Reading the clinical pages

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| `•` in a level column | Standing order at that certification level |
| `MC` | Contact Medical Control first |
| Blank cell | Not authorized at that level |

Levels run **EMR | EMT | A | I | P**. Do not read a single cell on its own — the authority
rules (which tiers inherit, when `MC` applies, what a blank really excludes) are defined in
one place: **[How to Read the Protocols](protocols/front-matter/01 - How to Read the Protocols.md)**.
Clinical numbers on this site are references, not prescriptions: **your local protocols and
your medical director govern.**
