PEMS Regional Patient Care Protocols, Policies and Procedures Sedation Post Advanced Airway Version: January 2025 Sedation Post Advanced Airway 
CRITERIA • Any patient receiving invasive ventilatory support following endotracheal intubation or placement of an advanced airway including supraglottic airway devices.  PROTOCOL EMR Follow Universal Patient Care / Initial Patient Contact protocol. EMR EMT Confirm and monitor airway placement with continuous SpO2 and box-waveform EtCO2. EMT I Administer:   ADULT: Fentanyl (Sublimaze) 25 – 50 mcg IV/IO over 2 minutes or IN/IM   PEDIATRIC: Fentanyl (Sublimaze) 0.5 – 1 mcg/kg IV/IO over 2 minutes or IN/IM Maximum dose 25 mcg.   Repeat every 10 – 15 minutes as needed for patient safety and comfort.  I 
I If Fentanyl is not sufficient, administer:  ADULT: Midazolam (Versed) 2.5 mg IV/IO over 2 minutes or 5 mg IN/IM  PEDIATRIC: Midazolam (Versed) 0.1 mg/kg IV/IO over 2 minutes or 0.2 mg/kg IN/IM Maximum dose 2.5 mg IV/IO or 5 mg IN/IM   Repeat every 10 - 15 minutes as needed for patient safety and comfort.  I MC Contact Medical Control for guidance if unable to adequately manage patient agitation or discomfort. MC  PEARLS • Adjust ventilation rate or volume to maintain EtCO2 of 35 – 45 mmHg following advanced airway placement.  • Closely monitor for hypotension following intubation and sedation. Positive pressure ventilation increases the risk of pneumothorax and reduced preload, both of which can result in sudden drops in mean arterial pressure.  • Use suction to remove blood, secretions and vomitus as needed.  • Use lower doses of Fentanyl for opiate naïve, frail, low weight, hypotensive, hemodynamically labile, or elderly (> 65 years) patients.
